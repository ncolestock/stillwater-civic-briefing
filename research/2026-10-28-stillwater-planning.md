## What the record says

The Stillwater Planning Commission is scheduled to meet Wednesday, October 28, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. at 216 4th Street North.

Public hearing Case No. CD2026-042 is a Zoning Text Amendment request to increase allowable accessory square footage on lots greater than 1,000 square feet in area. Applicant Daniel Garrity seeks to construct a 1,000 square foot accessory structure on the property at 12210 McKusick Rd in addition to a proposed attached garage. Current regulations restrict the footprint of an attached garage plus all accessory structures to 1,000 square feet (or ten percent of the net developable area of the lot, whichever is less). An accessory structure is limited to a maximum of 500 square feet, or 720 square feet when no garage is attached to the primary structure. Staff proposes to allow accessory structures of up to 1,000 square feet on parcels 50,000 square feet or greater in the Traditional Residential (TR) district, and to increase the maximum lot coverage for an attached garage and all accessory structures to 1,500 square feet for such lots. The applicant's property is approximately 1.7 acres. Only three parcels would be impacted. Date of complete application submission September 21, 2026. 60 Day Action Deadline November 20, 2026.

New business includes Case No. CD2026-046, an appeal of interpretation regarding accessory structures in the Cove Cottage Residential zoning district. Homeowners Gina and Dan Sedlacek appeal the staff determination that a pool is an accessory structure and therefore not allowed at 1234 Macey Way in Settler's Glen. City Code Sec. 28-322(g)(1) states that in CCR districts accessory structures are not allowed. Staff opinion is that a pool is an accessory structure. Three existing pools in Settlers Glen were approved administratively in the last five years.

Comprehensive Plan Conversation – Land Use and Density. Staff will discuss land use and density for the 2050 Comprehensive Plan. The updated land use map must demonstrate that land anticipated to develop in the next 20 years can accommodate the growth forecasts assigned by the Met Council. HKGi will attend. For reference, 2025 System Statement Imagine Forecasts: Population 19,394 (2020 actual) to 22,500 (2050); Households 7,750 to 9,600; Employment 7,974 to 12,500.

The items are scheduled for the meeting. Not yet voted.
