## What the record says

The Oakdale Environmental Management Commission meets October 19, 2026. The agenda includes a Land and Water Legacy Program presentation by Serena Raths and Emily Jorgensen of Washington County, covering program background, accomplishments, and a proposed 2026 bond referendum. The meeting has not happened.

## Dollars

Land and Water Legacy Program: Created in November 2006 with $20 million voter-approved referendum for land and water protection. Over $53 million invested ($19 million county funds, $34 million partnership funds). 2026 Bond Referendum: $40 million general obligation bonds proposed for November 3, 2026 election; estimated cost $24.60/year for 20 years on median residential homestead ($420,900 market value), $3 per $100,000 taxable value for agricultural homestead, $6 per $100,000 for agricultural non-homestead.
