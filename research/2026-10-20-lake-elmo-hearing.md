## What the record says

The Lake Elmo City Council will hold a public hearing Tuesday, October 20, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. at Lake Elmo City Hall, 3880 Laverne Avenue North, Lake Elmo, MN 55042.

The hearing considers two items: Old Lake Elmo Elementary Grading Permit and Business Subsidy Policy.

Stillwater Area Schools, ISD 834, has submitted a demolition and grading permit application for the property located at 11030 Stillwater Boulevard North. The request is to permit demolition of the old school building, removal of existing structure(s), parking, drive and other related improvements, earthwork, and vegetative restoration on the property. This work will involve the movement of approximately 525 cubic yards per acre of earth on site.

The City of Lake Elmo is holding a public hearing to consider a business subsidy policy pursuant to Minnesota Statute Sections 116J.993 through 116J.995. The policy will set a specific criterion with minimum requirements and standards that recipients must meet in order to receive a business subsidy.
