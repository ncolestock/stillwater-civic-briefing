## What the record says

The Minnesota Department of Transportation project page for the Hwy 243 Osceola Bridge, Franconia Township and Osceola, prints the bridge closure as scheduled. Under Bridge closures the page prints Late October 2026 through fall 2027, "The Hwy 243 Osceola Bridge over the St. Croix River is closed." Detours printed: Hwy 8 Taylors Falls River Crossing (north of Hwy 243) via Hwy 95, Hwy 8, Hwy 35 and Hwy 243; alternate via the Hwy 36 Stillwater River Crossing (south of Hwy 243) using Hwy 95, Hwy 36, Hwy 64, Hwy 35, Hwy 64B, Hwy 35 and Hwy 243.

Current facility closure printed: 8 a.m. Mon, Sept. 14 through late summer 2028, the Osceola Landing north boat launch and parking lot is closed.

The page says MnDOT is working with the Wisconsin Department of Transportation to replace the bridge over the St. Croix National and Scenic Riverway. The current bridge was built in 1953. No page date or last-updated line is printed. A Sept. 28, 2026 briefing of the same project page recorded the bridge closure line as mid-October 2026. This briefing records the line as printed on Oct. 4, 2026.
