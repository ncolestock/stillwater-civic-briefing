## What the record says

Washington County Board of Commissioners is scheduled to meet Tuesday, November 3, 2026, with the regular meeting starting at approximately 10:00 a.m. (times listed are approximate). The agenda includes a consent calendar with resolutions to act as state aid sponsor for several Local Road Improvement Program (LRIP) 2026 grant applications, approval of State of Minnesota Department of Natural Resources Grant Agreement No. 297766, a letter of support for a proposed property exchange and statutory decommissioning between the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources (MnDNR) and the National Park Service (NPS) for the Fairy Falls Day Use Area and the St. Croix Boom Site Wayside, and other contract amendments and grants. A board workshop with administration follows on Washington County’s 2027 Proposed Fee Schedule, Policy 1032. The meeting has not occurred. Items are scheduled or recommended on the agenda.

## Dollars

Grant Agreement No. 297766 with the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources: $1,244,000 from the Environment and Natural Resources Trust Fund for the Hardwood Creek Regional Trail Extension. (Consent Calendar item K.)

Purchase Order 28935: $211,494.98 with Insight Public Sector Inc. for network infrastructure. (Consent Calendar item E.)

Grant Agreement No. 18627 with the Minnesota Department of Public Safety: $63,100 for the Enforcement Grant Program for the period of October 23, 2026, through September 30, 2027. (Consent Calendar item N.)

Donation in the amount of $5,000 from Joshua’s Hope. (Consent Calendar item R.)

Amendment No. 1 to Contract No. 18127 with Washington County Community Development Agency for Bridges II housing program: adding $310,272 (total $465,408). (Consent Calendar item B.)

Amendment No. 1 to Contract No. 17004 for a Grant Agreement with the Minnesota Department of Human Services for 2027-2028 Mobile Crisis Grant funding: adding $1,382,956. (Consent Calendar item D.)

Amendment No. 3 to Agreement No. 13740 with Hennepin County for Metropolitan County Consortium Non-Emergency Medical Transportation services: adding a 10% administrative fee ($7,777 for 2027). (Consent Calendar item C.)

FY26 Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant Program application: $10,084 for CBRN air-purifying respirator filters. (Consent Calendar item S.)

2027 Proposed Fee Schedule, Policy 1032 (workshop discussion, changes effective January 1, 2027): black/white photocopies from 10¢ to 15¢ per page; color photocopies from 70¢ to 50¢ per page. Public Health regulatory program fees 5% increase. Other changes include Parks vehicle permits, camping, pavilion and facility rentals; Sheriff civil process fees and fingerprint cards from $15 to $20; temporary access permits for Public Works Transportation ($150 individual residential, $200 utility corridors). Public hearing scheduled for November 10, 2026. (Packet workshop section.)

Amount not in the document for several other consent calendar amendments and agreements.
