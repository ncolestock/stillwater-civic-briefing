## What the record says

Stillwater City Council is scheduled for a Workshop & Regular Meeting on November 4, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. Items include a public hearing on the Myrtle Street Improvement Project feasibility report and resolution to order improvements; a public hearing and resolution adopting assessments for a service charge to businesses in the Downtown Special Service District; consent items including return of excess increment from Tax Increment Financing (Redevelopment) District #8; and new business on a zoning text amendment for accessory structures in the Traditional Residential (TR) District (first reading), the 2027 World Snow Celebration event agreement with fee waiver request, noise variance, and temporary liquor license, and the 2027 Ice Rink in Parking Lot 4 agreement with fee waiver request. An official notice of the 2027 tipping fee is listed under council request items.

## Dollars

Myrtle Street Improvement Project (from Owens Street North to Main Street North, 0.72 miles): total estimated cost $4,570,142 (includes design, surveying, inspection, administration, contingencies). Assessments $670,142 (35% to benefiting property owners, 65% City-funded per Assessment Policy). State LRIP Grant $1,400,000. State Aid Funds $2,500,000. Residential assessment $7,804 per unit (unit method; corner lots at half unit per frontage; duplexes/condominiums at 0.8 units). Commercial, City, and Churches-owned properties $185 per adjusted linear foot. Appraisal estimated $12,000 benefit per residential unit. (Feasibility report.)

Downtown Special Service District service charge: estimated cost of services $50,500, which is $500 per commercial building within the District. Total amount $50,500.00. Each listed property assessed at $500.00 (one at $1,000.00). Payable with taxes in 2026, one annual installment with zero interest. Payment deadline November 16, 2026; remaining balance sent to the County as a special assessment. (Assessment memo and resolution.)

Tax Increment Financing (Redevelopment) District #8: excess increment $291,897.91 to be remitted to Washington County for redistribution to local taxing entities. District decertified as of December 31, 2022. (Finance memo.)

2027 World Snow Celebration (North Lowell Park, January 11 to January 17, 2027): total estimated fees $39,150 (weather dependent). Breakdown includes Special Event with Contract Application Fee $100; Event Setup Fee (7 days) $3,500 ($500/day); Event Base Fee (7 days) $3,500 ($500/day); Parking Lot 4 (14 days) $2,800 ($200/day); Parking Lot 5 (14 days) $2,800 ($200/day); Parking Lot 9 (14 days) $2,800 ($200/day); Parking Lot 10 (up to 25 days, snowmaking only) $5,000 ($200/day); Parking Lot 11 (up to 25 days, snowmaking only) $5,000 ($200/day); Water for Snowmaking $7,200 ($2/10,000 gallons); Alcohol Permit (6 days) $6,000 ($1,000/day); Food Service Vendors $300 ($50/day); Liquor Licenses Application Fee $150 ($25/day); Security Deposit $1,000 (on file, net $0). Greater Stillwater Chamber of Commerce requests waiver of all fees. (Event agreement.)

2027 Ice Rink in Parking Lot 4 (December 21, 2026, to March 22, 2027): fee waiver request for $100 Special Event Fee with Contract Application Fee and $18,400 Lot 4 Rental ($200/day for 92 days). Total fees requested for waiver $18,500. Security deposit $1,000. (Agreement.)

Ramsey/Washington Recycling & Energy Center tipping fee for acceptable waste: $159.00 per ton in 2027. This fee represents a 12% increase over 2026. (Official notice.)

Amount not in the document for Payment of Bills (consent).
