## What the record says

Stillwater City Council meets November 4, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. for a regular meeting. Items include a public hearing on the Myrtle Street Improvement Project feasibility report and resolution, a public hearing on proposed assessments for the Downtown Special Service District service charge, and a first reading of a zoning text amendment for accessory structures in the Traditional Residential district.

## Dollars

Myrtle Street Improvement Project total estimated cost is $4,570,142. Assessments $670,142. State LRIP Grant $1,400,000. State Aid Funds $2,500,000. Preliminary assessment rates: residential $7,804 per unit (78.6 units estimated); commercial, city-owned, churches $185 per adjusted linear foot (2,132 feet estimated). Appraised benefit $12,000 per residential unit.

Downtown Special Service District service charge total estimated cost $50,500 annually. $500 per commercial building (one parcel at $1,000).

Zoning amendment: no dollar figures.
