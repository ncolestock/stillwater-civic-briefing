## What the record says

The Washington County Board of Commissioners is scheduled Tuesday, October 6, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. The agenda footer says assistive listening devices are available in the County Board Room. The items below are on the agenda. They are not minutes of a vote already taken.

Consent Calendar, roll-call vote, includes approval of the September 15, 2026, County Board meeting minutes; appointment of Liz Jennings to a partial term on the Workforce Development Board as a DEED Representative starting October 7, 2026, and expiring December 31, 2028; a needs determination application for Empower Me Group Inc. to increase capacity for their existing Day Support Services Program; State of Minnesota Department of Commerce Grant Agreement No. SPB26-008 (Washington County Contract No. 18596) for Solar on Park Grove Library; a donation from Kwik Trip Hugo for the Sheriff's Office Annual Employee Appreciation Event; Amendment No. 1 to Contract No. 14328 with Hennepin County Sheriff's Office for inmate housing; and Amendment No. 1 to Joint Powers Agreement No. 15025 between the Sheriff's Office and the City of Forest Lake for sharing law enforcement data.

The packet on the solar grant says Washington County submitted an application June 30, 2026, and was awarded the grant August 24, 2026, to install a solar array on Park Grove Library. Public Works recommends approval of the grant agreement. Upon Board approval, the Public Works Director will approve a pending contract with Apadana Solar Technologies, contingent upon acceptance of the grant. Previous action printed: 6/23/2026, the Board adopted a resolution to submit a grant request.

The packet on the inmate-housing amendment says the original agreement is in effect through October 11, 2026. This amendment will extend the agreement through December 31, 2027. Hennepin County will pay a per diem daily rate for each inmate in custody. Washington County is under no obligation to lease jail beds to Hennepin County if there is no space. The original agreement was approved by the County Board on September 28, 2021.

After adjournment the agenda lists a 10:05 board workshop with Public Health and Environment on an Aquatic Invasive Species Program Update; a 10:25 workshop with Public Works to review the Glacial Hills Regional Trail Long-Range Plan; an 11:00 workshop with Administration to review a draft Climate Action Plan; and a 12:00 Finance Committee.

Also scheduled: a resolution to recognize October 2026 as Cybersecurity Month (Adam Larson, Information Technology Director) and a resolution honoring all Washington County employees and declaring October 5-9, 2026, Customer Service Week (Amy Stenftenagel, Property Records and Taxpayer Services Director).
