## What the record says

The Lake Elmo City Council regular meeting is scheduled Tuesday, October 6, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. at 3880 Laverne Avenue North. A separate special meeting the same night is a different agenda and is not this briefing.

The regular agenda lists a consent section and a regular section. The regular items are scheduled, not voted. Staff recommendations are printed in the packet memos.

Regular item 1 is a public hearing, "Frattalone Grading Permit," with consulting planner Nathan Fuerst presenting. The public hearing notice, dated for Tuesday, October 6th, 2026, at 7:00 p.m., at Lake Elmo City Hall, 3880 Laverne Avenue North, says the council "will be holding a public hearing to consider the following item" titled "Frattalone Grading Permit." The packet staff report titles it "PUBLIC HEARING – Frattalone Grading Permit Proposal" and recommends approval of the grading permit for PID 14.029.21.41.0030.

The report lists the applicant and owner as Frattalone Development, Inc., a subsidiary of Frattalone Construction, at 3205 Spruce St, St. Paul, MN 55117. Location: 10830 Stillwater Blvd. N. (PID# 14.029.21.41.0030). Request: Grading Permit (over 400 cubic yards/acre). Land use and zoning: V-LDR (Village – Low Density Residential). Application complete 9/1/2026. 60-day deadline 10/31/2026.

The report says the applicant proposes to bring in fill and grade on site approximately 60,000 cubic yards (2727 cubic yards/acre). It says there are no plans for future development associated with this request, so the property would remain undeveloped following the grading project. The site is described as historically the Schiltgen Farm South site. The report says some parts of the site would reach a maximum of 10' above the present elevation at the eastern lot boundary which borders existing residential uses. It says the applicant demolished the existing single-family home and associated structures in spring of 2026, except the farmstead barn, and is now proposing removal of the existing white barn seen from Stillwater Blvd. N. Grading permits over 400 cubic yards per acre require a public hearing and City Council approval. Standards are cited as LEC 105.08.060 (j). The report says there are not any known wetlands on this property. Klondike Avenue "will be required to be realigned with a future development on this property." Staff propose limiting work to April 1 through November 1, with final restoration by November 1, 2027.

Regular item 2 is "Approve Resolution Receiving Feasibility Report And Ordering Hearing On The Improvement For The 2027 Street And Utility Improvements," city engineer Nathan Stanley presenting. Staff recommend Resolution No. 2026-070. The printed hearing notice sets the improvement hearing for Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at or approximately after 7:00 p.m. The project is approximately 10,100 feet (1.9 miles) of public streets: Hidden Bay Tr N from Ideal Ave N (CSAH 13) to Birchbark Trail N; Jack Pine Tr N from Windbreak Tr N to approximately 70' north of the Jack Pine Tr N and Deer Pond Tr N intersection; Deer Pond Tr N from Hidden Bay Tr N to approximately 270' north of that intersection; Windbreak Tr N from Hidden Bay Tr N to Deer Pond Tr N; Hidden Bay Ct from Hidden Bay Tr N to the northwest end of the cul-de-sac; Cherrywood Tr N from Hidden Bay Tr N to Birchbark Tr N; Birchbark Tr N from Hidden Bay Tr N to the south end of the cul-de-sac. Work described: full-depth reclamation, spot subgrade correction, concrete curb and gutter, new bituminous surface, ditch regrading, drain tile, and storm sewer. Roadways are printed as 20-22 feet wide. Hidden Bay Trail from Deer Pond Trail (south intersection) to Deer Pond Trail (north intersection) is recommended to be widened to a consistent 22-feet wide where feasible, a widening of 1-2 feet in select locations. Street and drainage improvements are assessed at 30 percent of total project costs for residential properties, over 15 years, at an interest rate of 1 percent over the bond rate.

Regular item 3 is the same action for the 2027 Collector Road Improvements, also Nathan Stanley presenting, with the improvement hearing printed for the November 4th, 2026 council meeting. Segments: 45th Street N, from Jane Road N to Julep Ave N; Julep Ave N, from 45th Street N to 47th Street N; 47th Street N, from Julep Ave N to Keats Ave N. Work described: full depth reclamation and new bituminous pavement, minor storm sewer maintenance, ditch grading, and draintile. "No street widening or geometric changes are proposed."

Regular item 4 is "TIF Resolution 11030 Stillwater Blvd N," community development director Stopa presenting. Staff recommend Resolution NO. 2026-071. The memo says adoption does not require the city to create a TIF district. LHB inspected the site on 9/18/2026 and found it meets the criteria for a Redevelopment TIF. The resolution identifies parcels at 11030 Stillwater Blvd N., PINs 1302921230001, 1302921230002, 1302921230003, 1302921220004, and 1302921220001. The pre-demolition report is dated October 1, 2026. The memo says the school district has submitted grading and demolition permits and would like to start interior demolition in October. A later grading public hearing for 11030 Stillwater Blvd N is listed for 10/20, with demolition of the old school after that meeting. A business subsidy workshop is listed for 10/13 and adoption for 10/20.

Regular item 5 is "Screaton Property Agreement," contract planner Nathan Fuerst presenting. Staff recommend Resolution 2026-068. The memo says the July 21, 2026 council approved the preliminary plat and PUD for Limerick Village and allowed a nonstandard access to the Screaton Property for an existing farming operation that relies on access to grain silos, and delayed a trail in the northeast corner. The agreement is with David Screaton (Screaton-Lee Family Trust). Printed terms: drainage conveyance stays in place until 2040, or earlier if the property is split or subdivided; the trail section is not constructed until 2040, or earlier on the same trigger; the 5th Street segment from Manning to the access remains posted at the current policy (10 tons outside of 7-ton spring weight restrictions) until 2040, or earlier on the same trigger.

Regular item 6 is "Purchase CIP Project F-012 – Ladder 1 Replacement," fire chief Dustin Kalis presenting. The memo says the adopted 2026-2035 Capital Improvement Plan included replacement of Ladder 1 in 2029. Staff ask the council to authorize a contract with Rosenbauer Minnesota, LLC, using Sourcewell, not to exceed $1,915,000.00.

Regular item 7 is a Lake Elmo Baseball Association donation for the VFW ballfield, assistant public works director Swanepoel presenting. Regular item 8 is a 3rd quarter goals update.

Consent items on the agenda include payments and disbursements; the 2027 LMCIT waiver form and renewal; a resolution of municipal support for CSAH 10/CSAH 17/CR 17B intersection improvements (preferred design printed as a single-lane roundabout, construction planned to begin in 2028, Resolution 2026-069 recommended); cooperative agreement payment #2 for TH36-Lake Elmo Avenue improvements; Primrose Daycare security reduction; 2026 seal coat pay request no. 1 (final); Gardner School site improvements warranty security reduction at 9880 Hudson Blvd; Elmo's Pup Park parking lot pay request #1; CSAH 14 watermain pay request #4; a 2026 trail maintenance contract; a criminal prosecution contract with Eckberg Lammers for 2027-2029; hiring of lead workers Monroe and Lopez; recruitment of a 2027 public works operator; a Lake Elmo Baseball Association donation for Ridge Park; and an Xcel municipality streetlight attachment agreement.

The agenda prints that members may participate remotely under Minn. Stat. Section 13D.02.
