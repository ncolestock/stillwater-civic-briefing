## What the record says

The Lake Elmo public hearing notice is dated Tuesday, October 6th, 2026, at 7:00 p.m., at Lake Elmo City Hall, 3880 Laverne Avenue North, Lake Elmo MN 55042. The Lake Elmo City Council "will be holding a public hearing to consider the following item."

The notice titles the item "Frattalone Grading Permit." It states: "Frattalone Companies has submitted a grading permit application for the property located at 10830 Stillwater Boulevard North." "The reason for this request is to permit the movement approximately 60,000 cubic yards (2727 cubic yards per acre) of earth on site." "The request includes importing of fill, earthwork, and construction of stormwater improvements on the property."

The notice says the City Council agenda and packet items relating to this application "will be made available prior to the meeting" on the city website under Government > Agendas & Minutes > City Council. It says the public may attend in person to speak, and that handwritten comments may be submitted by mail no later than 5:00 p.m. the Wednesday before the meeting, or emailed to Sophia Jensen no later than noon on the day of the meeting.

The item is scheduled as a public hearing. No recommended vote language is printed on the notice.
