## What the record says

The Washington County Library Board is scheduled Monday, Sept. 28, 2026, from 6:30 to 8:00 p.m. at R.H. Stafford Library (Woodbury), Savannah A (lower level) inside Central Parks.

The agenda is: call to order and recognition of the public; consent (agenda and July 27, 2026 minutes); Strategic Facilities Plan workshop with MSR Design; Park Grove / R.H. Stafford libraries construction update by Erik Jalowitz, Capital Project Manager; 2027 budget presentation by Jacquie Kramer, Library Director; statistics and updates by Kramer; adjourn.

The facilities-plan slides name Capital Improvement Plan item BSD-LIB-2601, a contract with MSR Design approved July 14, 2026, a 15-year outlook, 7 branch locations, the Law Library, and 3 Library Express locker locations.

July 27, 2026 minutes in the packet: Patnaik called the meeting to order at 6:33 p.m. Present board: Bigham, Cosby, Olufson, Patnaik, Seim, Williams. Absent: Fink, Jansen. Staff: De Ruyter, Hall, Jalowitz, Kramer. Williams moved and Cosby seconded adoption of the agenda and May 18, 2026 minutes; the motion passed unanimously. Jalowitz reported Park Grove demolition complete, entry foundation laid, underground plumbing and electrical installed, utilities underway, and water damage that led to replacing all exterior walls while retaining structural steel and roof. R.H. Stafford remained in design, including a pre-design feasibility study with MSR Design.
