## What the record says

The Oakdale City Council is scheduled to meet Tuesday, November 10, 2026 at 7:00 PM at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Ave N. Items include canvassing the November 3, 2026 general election returns, a resolution of support for the County Highway 13 improvement project, initiation of 2027 street reconstruction and overlay projects with a feasibility report, approval of claims, and other consensus motions. The packet includes workshop minutes from October 27, 2026 on utility rates.

## Dollars

Claims for the period October 28, 2026 to November 10, 2026 in the amount of $2,838,563.15.

Agreement for Professional Services with SEH Inc. for feasibility report not to exceed $44,112.

Donation of $50.00 Dunkin’ gift card for Fire Department.
