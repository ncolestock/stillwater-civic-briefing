## What the record says

Lake Elmo City Council is scheduled for a regular meeting at 7:00 p.m. on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at City of Lake Elmo, 3880 Laverne Avenue North. A special meeting is also scheduled the same day for a closed session to discuss matters protected by the attorney-client privilege pursuant to Minnesota Statutes, Section 13D.05, Subd. 3(b) relating to the pending contested case hearing between the City of Lake Elmo and the DNR relating to amendments to the City’s Water Appropriations Permit.

The regular agenda includes consent items: approval of payments and disbursements, acceptance of donations to the Fire Department from Dairy Queen and Jack's Shack Concessions, approval of Cooperative Agreement Payment #3 for TH36-Lake Elmo Avenue Improvements, approval of Pay Request #3 (FINAL) for Test Well 6, acceptance of improvements and approval of Pay Request No. 4 (FINAL) for the Stillwater Area School District Utility Improvements, approval of warranty security release for Springs at Lake Elmo, approval of security release for MEP Lake Elmo LLC (Milestones Daycare Center), approval of security release for the Savona 4th Addition Project, approval of letter of support for Washington County’s Local Road Improvement Program (LRIP) Grant Application for the CSAH 10/CSAH 17/CR 17B Intersection Improvements, approval of Resolution of Municipal Support for CSAH 13 Improvements, authorization of contract for 2027 Water Supply Plan Update, approval of plans and specifications and order advertisement for bids for the 2026/2027 Crosswalk Improvements, approval of hire of Public Works Operator, approval of 2027 Compensation Policy, and approval of sale of excess and retired assets.

Regular agenda items: Old Lake Elmo Elementary Grading and Demolition Permit Proposal (Consulting Planner Nathan Fuerst presenting); Public Improvement Hearing and Approve Resolution Ordering the Improvement for the 2027 Street and Utility Improvements (City Engineer Nate Stanley presenting); Public Improvement Hearing and Approve Resolution Ordering the Improvement for the 2027 Collector Road Improvements (City Engineer Nate Stanley presenting); Approve City Comments for Submission Regarding the Washington County Draft 2027-2031 Capital Improvement Plan (City Engineer Nate Stanley presenting).

## Dollars

2027 Street and Utility Improvements (Oace Acres Neighborhood, 1.9 miles): total estimated project cost $2,897,000 (includes construction pricing, contingencies, engineering design, and construction service fees). Funding: Infrastructure Reserve Fund 409 $319,000; bond proceeds $1,705,800; special assessments $872,200. Assessment rate 30% of total project costs for residential properties using a unit method; total preliminary assessment $872,200 for 89 units, equating to $9,800 per unit. Design contract with Bolton & Menk, Inc.: not-to-exceed $145,200 for final design and bidding phase. From council memo.

2027 Collector Road Improvements (45th Street N from Jane Road N to Julep Ave N; Julep Ave N from 45th Street N to 47th Street N; 47th Street N from Julep Ave N to Keats Ave N; total 0.74 miles): total estimated project cost $950,000. Total proposed assessments $864,200 ($6,600 for 13 units); city share $85,800. From council memo.

2027 Water Supply Plan Update: $17,590 not-to-exceed contract with SEH, funded through the 2026 Water Fund. From council memo.

2026/2027 Crosswalk Improvements: estimated budget $145,000. From council memo.

2027 Compensation Policy: salary ranges effective January 1, 2027, Grade 1 $41,575.74 to $55,874.32 annually ($19.99 to $26.86 hourly) through Grade 15 $157,885.71 to $212,185.19 annually ($75.91 to $102.01 hourly). 34% increase from minimum to maximum step within each grade. From staff report.

Amount not in the document for Cooperative Agreement Payment #3, Pay Request #3 for Test Well 6, Pay Request No. 4 for Stillwater Area School District Utility Improvements, security releases, donations, payments and disbursements total, LRIP letter of support, CSAH 13 resolution, Public Works Operator hire, and asset sale.
