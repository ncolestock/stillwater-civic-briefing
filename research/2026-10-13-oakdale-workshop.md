## What the record says

The Oakdale City Council workshop is scheduled October 13, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Ave N. The agenda lists discussion items. Staff is seeking feedback or direction. No vote is printed.

5:00 p.m.: Oakdale Summerfest Community Organization (OSCO) update. The memorandum says the organization voted to continue Summerfest if security and funding details can be addressed. Larry Eberhard, Mary Koonce, and Pam Jungmann are listed to discuss 2026 successes and challenges and 2027 requirements and budget. Council direction on participation and budgeting is requested. A 2027 dollar figure is not in the memorandum.

5:30 p.m.: Helmo Station Metropolitan Council transit-oriented development site, joint powers agreement. The Metropolitan Council owns an approximately 8-acre site within the Helmo Station Planned Unit Development adjacent to the Gold Line bus rapid transit station. The property was acquired for the Gold Line project and previously contained a warehouse, which has been demolished. An approximately 1-acre park-and-ride is built on the southern portion, leaving approximately seven acres for redevelopment and supporting infrastructure. At the December 9, 2025 workshop, council members directed staff to keep the Helmo Station Planned Unit Development as the framework and proceed with concept plan development. Staff now seeks feedback on draft joint-powers terms among the city, the city Economic Development Authority, and the Metropolitan Council. The memorandum says the agreement does not approve a specific development, developer, financing package, or property conveyance. The city would still control zoning and development approvals. Any future project would still need the city's normal approval process, including final council approval. The selected developer will pay market value for the property to the Metropolitan Council unless otherwise approved consistent with legal and funding-source requirements. The agreement would not obligate the city to fund future infrastructure. Staff's printed timeline: October 2026, revision and finalization of terms; November 10, 2026, city council consideration and approval of the agreement.

5:50 p.m.: Tanners Lake Park. Staff seeks direction on the scope of 2027 improvements, a playground theme ("Under the Dock" and a second theme named in the memorandum), and lighting. The 2024 Lighting Study by SEH is cited. The city uses a 13-foot post-top unit with a concrete pole and King-branded Acorn luminaire. On average those fixtures are placed 240 feet apart, which the memorandum calls non-continuous lighting. Continuous lighting would need more fixtures and higher costs. The Acorn fixture is not DarkSky Approved because of its lens. A prior lighting cost estimate is described as being for non-continuous lighting; the dollar amount of that estimate is not reprinted in the memorandum pages opened.

6:10 p.m.: proposed updates to City Policy CC-002, City Council Advisory Boards and Commissions, and a meaningful-work discussion. A later attachment prints current pay as $25 per meeting for the chairperson and $20 for each board member, and a draft line that reads "$2517" for the chairperson and "$2015" for each board member. That draft line is printed without a dollar sign between the digits.

6:30 p.m. administrator update. 6:35 p.m. council topics. 6:45 p.m. adjourn.
