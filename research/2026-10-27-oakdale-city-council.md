## What the record says

The Oakdale City Council is scheduled to meet Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at 7:00 PM at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Ave N. The agenda includes a public hearing on certification of delinquent utilities and inactive accounts to Washington County for inclusion on 2027 property taxes (Resolution 2026-79). Consensus motions include approving Police Department Lexipol policies, adopting Resolution 2026-78 authorizing closure of city debt service funds, authorizing an agreement for professional services with KLM Engineering, Inc. for City Project U2026-04 (Water Tower 4 Reconditioning) in the amount of $141,280, and executing a Joint Powers Agreement with the City of Lake Elmo allowing the property at 4455 Olson Lake Trail to connect to Oakdale’s sanitary sewer system. Other items include a proclamation for First Responders Day, advisory board reports, and staff reports including a Memorandum of Understanding with Ramsey-Washington Metro Watershed District for sediment removal.
