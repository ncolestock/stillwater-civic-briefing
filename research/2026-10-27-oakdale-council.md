## What the record says

Oakdale City Council is scheduled to meet October 27, 2026 at 7:00 PM at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Ave N, Oakdale, Minnesota. Items include a public hearing on certification of delinquent utilities and inactive accounts to Washington County for inclusion on 2027 property taxes (Resolution 2026-79), consensus motions on police policies, closure of city debt service funds (Resolution 2026-78), professional services agreement for Water Tower 4 Reconditioning, joint powers agreement with Lake Elmo for sanitary sewer at 4455 Olson Lake Trail, memorandum of understanding with Ramsey-Washington Metro Watershed District, change order for police expansion and city hall remodel, and approval of claims.
