## What the record says

Stillwater Area Public Schools School Board meets November 10, 2026, at 5:30 PM at Oak Park Learning Center, 6355 Osman Avenue North, Stillwater, MN 55082, for a Canvass Election and Study Session. Business items include action on High School Course Proposal for the 2027-28 school year at Stillwater Area High School, including expanded college credit opportunities, career pathways and online learning options; Resolution Canvassing Returns of Voters of School District General Election; and Resolution Authorizing Issuance of Certificates of the General Election. Workshop topic: Enrollment, Transportation and Child Care. Closed session pursuant to Minn. Stat. § 13D.05, subd. 3(c)(3), to develop or consider an offer to purchase or sell real property, with possible action based on the topics discussed.
