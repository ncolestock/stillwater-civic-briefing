## What the record says

Presented to the school board November 10, 2026. Old Lake Elmo Elementary School site consists of 5 different parcels totaling 12.86 acres. Valley Community Center terminated their purchased agreement on September 28. Since then, two offers received: Lander Group and Kwik Trip. Administrative Recommendation: Authorize the district administration to sign a letter of intent to sell 3 parcels to Kwik Trip for approximately $3,780,000. Administration will then work through our realtor to discuss options with Lander and VCC for the potential purchase of the remainder of the property. The document is from the November 10 meeting materials. Local news reports a letter of intent was signed earlier this month.

## Dollars

Lander Group Offer: Offer to purchase all 12.86 acres. Offer amount is $4,650,000. This is approximately $8.30 per square foot. Kwik Trip Offer: Offer to purchase the southern three parcels which is approximately 252,648 square feet. Offer to pay $15 per square foot. Offer amount is approximately $3,789,720. Administrative Recommendation: Authorize the district administration to sign a letter of intent to sell 3 parcels to Kwik Trip for approximately $3,780,000.
