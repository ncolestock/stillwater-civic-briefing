## What the record says

The Oakdale City Council is scheduled to meet Tuesday, November 24, 2026, at 7:00 PM at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Ave N. The agenda includes public hearings and actions on the Cedric’s Crossing development (comprehensive plan amendment, PUD amendment, preliminary plat, and site plan for a 55-unit rental townhome project), a zoning text amendment and variance request for a billboard in the Tanners Lake PUD (recommended for denial), claims approval, a proclamation for Small Business Saturday, consensus motions including used car dealer license renewals and appointments, a Metropolitan Council grant agreement, and staff reports. Workshop notes from November 10, 2026 discuss TIF district issues and CIP updates.

## Dollars

Claims for the period November 11, 2026 to November 24, 2026 in the amount of $3,350,906.95.

$55,000 contribution toward Highway 120 improvements as a condition of the Cedric’s Crossing site plan.

Feasibility report for 2027 street projects referenced up to $44,112 in prior materials.

Tanners Lake TIF District No. 2-12: $2.3 million surplus to be returned to taxing authorities (Washington County, ISD 622, City) by December 31, 2027, if decertified.

Metropolitan Council Livable Communities Act grant of $1,000,000 for Hudson Boulevard Apartments (72-unit affordable housing at 6700 Hudson Boulevard North); no local match; to be administered as deferred loan to developer.

Independent Contractor Agreement with Dallas Dosch at $45/hour.

Life Development Resources agreement for Oakdale Fire Department mental/behavioral health support anticipated under $10,000.

Warming House Attendants at $16/hour.

From workshop: 2027-2031 CIP with 3.8% property tax levy increase adjusted to 4.3%; utility rates increased 4%.

## Votes

Not yet voted. Planning Commission recommended approval of Cedric’s Crossing items on October 1, 2026, and denial of the billboard text amendment and variance. Staff recommendations to adopt resolutions and ordinances as listed.
