## What the record says

The Oakdale City Council is scheduled to meet Tuesday, November 24, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Ave N. The agenda includes a public hearing on a Comprehensive Plan Amendment, Planned Unit Development Amendment, Preliminary Plat, and Site Plan for Cedric’s Crossing, a 55-unit rental townhome development on approximately 44.6 acres (parcels 0602921230013 and 5585 Golfview Avenue North), the former Oakdale Par 3 golf course and firearms range. Staff recommend adopting Resolution 2026-86 (Comprehensive Plan Amendment from Open Space to Medium Density Residential), Ordinance No. 967 (PUD amendment), Resolution 2026-87 (Preliminary Plat), and Resolution 2026-88 (Site Plan). The developer is to contribute $55,000 toward future Highway 120 improvements. Other items include approval of claims for November 11–24, 2026 totaling $3,350,906.95; acceptance of a $1,000,000 Metropolitan Council Livable Communities Act grant for Hudson Boulevard Apartments; discussion of returning $2.3 million TIF surplus from decertified districts; initiation of City Projects R2027-01 (2027 Street Reconstruction) and R2027-02 (2027 Street Overlay) with a feasibility report authorization of $44,112 with SEH Inc.; used car dealer licenses; and zoning text amendments.

## Dollars

$55,000 contribution by the developer toward future Highway 120 improvements (left turn lane). Claims approval: $3,350,906.95 for November 11, 2026 to November 24, 2026. Metropolitan Council Livable Communities Act grant: $1,000,000 for Hudson Boulevard Apartments (72-unit multifamily affordable housing at 6700 Hudson Boulevard North), structured as a deferred no-interest loan, no local match. TIF surplus: $2.3 million from decertified TIF Districts (1-4 and 1-6) to be returned to taxing authorities (including ISD 622) by December 31, 2027, if no redevelopment. Feasibility report for street projects: $44,112 with SEH Inc. Park dedication fee for Cedric’s Crossing per City fee schedule (amount not specified in the reviewed portion). Engineering services noted in claims (e.g., $23,074.50, $60,591.95). Amount not in the document for some agreements.
