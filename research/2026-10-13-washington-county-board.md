## What the record says

The Washington County Board of Commissioners is scheduled to meet October 13, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. The agenda prints "All listed times are approximate. Board Workshops will start immediately following the conclusion of the Board meeting." The meeting is listed on the county calendar as 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. in Stillwater, MN 55082. Items are scheduled; none are recorded as already voted.

Commissioners listed: Fran Miron, District 1; Stan Karwoski, District 2; Bethany Cox, District 3; Chair, Karla Bigham, District 4; Michelle Clasen, District 5.

Consent calendar items (generally defined as routine business approved in one vote; commissioners may pull items): approval of the September 22, 2026, County Board meeting minutes; resolution appointing Matt Moore, City of Oakdale, to a first term beginning November 14, 2026, and expiring November 13, 2029, as Manager on the Valley Branch Watershed District; resolution reappointing Don Pereira, City of Afton, to a second full term on the same body for the same dates; approval to set a public hearing for November 10, 2026, to consider revisions to Washington County's 2027 Fee Schedule, Policy No. 1032; Approve Agreement No. 18614 with Hennepin County on behalf of the Metropolitan County Consortium to return funds to Washington County in the amount of $13,548; resolution certifying to the county auditor unpaid County Environmental Charges (CEC) and order the county auditor to extend the assessments with interest upon the tax rolls of the county; resolution to submit three projects to the Minnesota Department of Transportation’s Local Road Improvement Program (LRIP) 2026 grant solicitation; resolution to participate in the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources (DNR) Snowmobile Trails Assistance Program, and for staff to administer grant for the Star Trail Association and approve license agreement between the County and the Star Trail Association for the creation and maintenance of snowmobile trails within certain areas of the county parks; Approve Contract No. 18607 with HDR Engineering Inc. in the amount of $449,777.18 to provide final design services for the County Road 50 (202nd St N) and Trunk Highway 61 Intersection Improvement Project.

Public Works items: Adopt a resolution of support for the Glacial Hills Regional Trail Long-Range Plan to be submitted to the Metropolitan Council for review and approval (presenter Connor Schaefer, Senior Planner). Approve a Budget Amendment authorizing the Temporary Use of Fund Balance in the amount of $450,000 and Approve Contract No. 18615 with Pember Companies, Inc. in the amount of $1,083,920 for the LEC Combined Perimeter Upgrade Project (presenter Greg Miller, Building Services Project Manager, Senior).

General Administration: Presentation of the 2025 Annual Report on Community Indicators (ARCI) (Kevin Corbid, County Administrator; Brooke Crider, Planner I).

After adjournment, Board Workshop with Administration: Review and discussion of the proposed Washington County 2027 Legislative Platform. Board Workshop with Public Works: Review the draft 2027-2031 Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) and County Transportation Sales Tax Update.
