## What the record says

Washington County Board of Commissioners meets October 13, 2026, at 9:00 AM. Agenda includes consent calendar items and public works items scheduled for the meeting. Items include approval of Contract No. 18607 with HDR Engineering Inc. for final design services on the County Road 50 (202nd St N) and Trunk Highway 61 Intersection Improvement Project, a budget amendment and contract for the Law Enforcement Center combined perimeter upgrade project, a resolution of support for the Glacial Hills Regional Trail Long-Range Plan, and a resolution to participate in the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources Snowmobile Trails Assistance Program for the Star Trail Association.
