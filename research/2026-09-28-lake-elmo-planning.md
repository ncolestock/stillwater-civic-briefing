## What the record says

Lake Elmo Planning Commission is scheduled Monday, Sept. 28, 2026, at 6:30 p.m. at 3880 Laverne Avenue North.

Public hearings:

- 4455 Olson Lake Trail North, 2040 Comprehensive Plan amendment. Lisa Smith submitted the amendment. The agenda says the reason is a noncompliant septic system. The request includes expanding the city's MUSA boundary (served by Oakdale) and re-guiding the property to RSFS. Packet materials listed: presentation and PC memo.

- 11055 32nd Street, variance. Doug Biever submitted a variance at 11055 32nd St North for flexibility from LEC 105.12.630 Table 9-2, which requires that properties not exceed an impervious surface coverage of 25%. The applicant proposes to construct a new garage and relocate the driveway. Packet materials listed: PC memo and presentation.

New/unfinished business: Old Fire Hall and Parks buildings purchase. "The City intends to sell property in its old village (PIDs: 1302921320100 and 1302921320099). The Planning Commission shall review the proposed acquisition and report that the sale will comply with the city’s comprehensive plan." Documents listed include Planning Commission Resolution 9.28.2026 and a PC memo and presentation titled sale of fire station review.

Communications list Limerick Village preliminary plat and PUD (7/21/26) and MYP zoning text amendment and CUP (8/5/26). October 14, 2026, meeting canceled; next listed meeting October 26, 2026.
