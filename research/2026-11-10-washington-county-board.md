## What the record says

Washington County Board of Commissioners meets November 10, 2026, at 9:00 AM. Items include a public hearing on revisions to the Washington County Fee Schedule, Policy No. 1032; consent calendar items with contracts and a property purchase; an update on Election and Land & Water Legacy Referendum; a resolution establishing a process for any potential future consideration of Automated License Plate Readers; and a closed session to review a new appraisal for Phase II and Phase III of the Kelley Land and Cattle property for inclusion into Big Marine Park Reserve.

## Dollars

Consent Calendar item C: Approve Amendment No. 2 to Contract No. 17224 with Valley Outreach for homeless prevention services in the amount of $75,000, bringing the contract total not to exceed amount to $210,000, and extending the contract term to December 31, 2027.

Item D: Approve Contract No. 18657 with Homecare Software Solutions, LLC dba HHAeXchange in an amount of $330,000 for a term of November 13, 2026, through November 13, 2031.

Item E: Approve Grant Agreement No. 18642 with Minnesota Pollution Control Agency in the amount of $995,956.00 for the first year of funding for the grant term of July 1, 2026, through June 30, 3031, for waste reduction and recycling activities.

Item F: Approve Contract No. 18637 with WSB & Associates in the amount of $386,598 to provide final design services for the CSAH 10 and CSAH 17 Intersection Project.

Item G: Adopt a resolution for the purchase of property at 15085 62nd Street, in the City of Oak Park Heights for inclusion into the Government Center Campus. Amount not in the document.

Item H: Adopt a resolution to approve a donation from Our Rescue to support human trafficking investigations. Amount not in the document.
