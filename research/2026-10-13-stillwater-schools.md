## What the record says

The Stillwater Area Public Schools board study session is scheduled October 13, 2026, at 5:30 p.m. at Oak Park Learning Center, 6355 Osman Avenue North, Stillwater, MN 55082. The BoardBook listing calls the meeting type Regular and the title Study Session. Items are scheduled. The agenda does not print a staff recommendation to adopt a levy or a course list at this meeting.

Consent agenda: payment of invoices, September 19–October 9, 2026, and a Stillwater Middle School donation proposal. The invoice item says a copy of the payment register has been distributed to board members. The donation item is an Eagle Scout service project to build and donate wooden picnic benches to Stillwater Middle School, estimated donation value $800 for materials and supplies.

Business item: Part-Time Teacher Program resolution. Speaker: Kristine Carlston, executive director of human resources. The agenda says the resolution is included for review. The resolution text was not on the agenda page opened.

Workshop topics, each marked information only except the course-proposal item:

- Achievement update. Speakers: Caitlyn Willis, chief academic officer, and Elsa Mundt, educational data analyst. The description says Minnesota's Comprehensive Achievement and Civic Readiness legislation requires districts to set achievement goals, monitor progress, and report annually. The district will present its goals and progress, and 2025-26 student achievement results, including Minnesota Comprehensive Assessment results in reading, mathematics, and science. The agenda says this report is for information only. No scores are printed on the agenda page.
- High school schedule update. Speaker: Rob Bach, Stillwater Area High School principal. The description says Stillwater Area High School implemented a redesigned schedule for the 2026-27 school year to expand course offerings, increase flexibility, and provide more opportunities for students. The presentation will cover early implementation results, student participation in new programming, feedback, and opportunities for improvement. Information only. No participation counts are printed.
- High school course proposal process. Speaker: Caitlyn Willis. A discussion of the process and timeline. "A final list of the course proposals will be presented for your review and approval at the October business meeting."

Closed session: pursuant to Minn. Stat. § 13D.05, subd. 3(c)(3), to develop or consider an offer to purchase or sell real property. No address, price, or parcel is printed.

Chair Alison Sherman is listed as speaker for call to order, roll call, pledge, approval of the agenda, the closed session, and adjournment. Student report speakers: Avery Crickenberger and Katie Smith, student representatives.
