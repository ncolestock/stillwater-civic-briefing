## What the record says

Woodbury City Council is scheduled Wednesday, Sept. 23, 2026, at 7:40 p.m., or immediately after a Housing and Redevelopment Authority meeting, at Woodbury City Hall council chambers and by virtual link. The agenda lists public hearings and a discussion item that have not been voted.

Staff recommends the council adopt Interim Ordinance 2082 authorizing a study and imposing a moratorium on data centers and other high-volume public infrastructure users. Council Letter 26-186 says Minnesota Statutes, section 462.355, subdivision 4, authorizes an interim ordinance for a period not to exceed one year. The letter says there are no current or pending development applications for new data centers. It lists existing data-center addresses at 500, 515, and 401 Bielenberg Drive. It says the interim ordinance would pause additional building permits for construction of new facilities or expansion of the existing facilities listed.

Staff recommends approval of the 2025 Consolidated Annual Performance and Evaluation Report for federal CDBG and HOME grant funds (Resolution 26-109).

Staff recommends Ordinance 2081 enacting a recodified code of ordinances.

Staff and the Planning Commission (recommended approval 6-0 on Sept. 14, 2026) recommend rezoning, a planned unit development, a conditional use permit, and a first-phase preliminary plat for Nottingham, Project No. 26-2026-00614. Council Letter 26-189 says Pulte Homes of Minnesota, LLC applied. The rezoning and PUD cover four properties totaling 53 acres. The first-phase preliminary plat is 28 acres and 35 single-family lots. The property is located north of Dale Road and west of Cottage Grove Drive. It is zoned R-1, Urban Reserve District, and guided Low Density. The recommended rezoning is to R-4, Urban Residential District.

Discussion item 8A asks the council to approve a preliminary 2027 property tax levy and budget, cancel or change certain ad valorem debt levies, approve a preliminary HRA special-benefit tax levy, and confirm a Truth-in-Taxation public meeting for Wednesday, Dec. 9, 2026, at 7:30 p.m.

Consent includes a Washington County cooperative agreement for limited right-of-way use on CSAH 13 (Radio Drive) for the public-safety building expansion; cooperative agreements for a traffic signal on CSAH 16 (Valley Creek Road) at Eagle Creek Lane; an all-way stop on Settlers Ridge Parkway at Eastview Road; a lawful-gambling application for Destination Education a Scholarship Foundation at Mark's Café & Bar, 10060 City Walk Drive; a Precinct 13 polling-place change; appointment of election judges for the Nov. 3, 2026, general election; minutes; and the abstract of bills.

Open Forum speakers are limited to three minutes. Public-hearing comments may not exceed 10 minutes each, with an aggregate thirty-minute public comment period described on the agenda.
