## What the record says

At approximately 6:11 am on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, the Cottage Grove Police Department and the Washington County Sheriff’s Office jointly responded to a report of shots fired within Ravine Park in Cottage Grove, MN.

Upon arrival, officers met a witness who reported a shooting. Officers found two individuals with gunshot wounds, who were pronounced deceased on scene.

The preliminary investigation indicates that these individuals were known to each other and that this was not a random act of violence. There is no active threat to the community.

This incident was contained to Ravine Park, and responding agencies established a perimeter around the park. Currently, Ravine Park remains closed until further notice. The Washington County Service Center will remain closed for the day. Cottage Grove City Hall and the HERO Center have reopened.

The Ramsey County Medical Examiner’s Office will be responsible for identification and notification to next of kin.
