## What the record says

The Washington County Board of Commissioners adopted a resolution Nov. 10 to purchase the property at 15085 62nd Street in the City of Oak Park Heights for inclusion in the Washington County Government Center Campus.

The Government Center Campus in Stillwater includes the Government Center, Law Enforcement Center, Courthouse, Emergency Housing Services Building, and parking ramp. Over the years, the county has acquired adjacent properties as they became available to consolidate, secure, and support long-term development of the campus.

The County Board reviewed the proposed acquisition in closed session on Sept. 15, 2026. The board determined that the property would provide long-term value by supporting future growth of the campus. Following negotiations, the county and the property owners agreed on a purchase price of $357,700.
