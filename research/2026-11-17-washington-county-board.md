## What the record says

The Washington County Board of Commissioners meets Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. in the County Board Room at the Government Center, 14949 62nd Street North, Stillwater.

The agenda includes public hearings on the 2027-2031 Capital Improvement Plan and the issuance of General Obligation Capital Improvement Bonds under the plan, and on editing the project eligibility list associated with the County Transportation Sales Tax.

The board is also scheduled to approve Washington County's 2027 Fee Schedule, Policy No. 1032, discuss proposed 2027 property taxes, and review recommended adjustments to the 2027 proposed budget.

Consent items include approval of a short-term lease for property at 5879 220th Street North in Forest Lake, a $1,000 donation from Hugo Yellow Ribbon Network, a $3,500 donation from Karen Marchetti for a bench at Big Marine Park Reserve, and use of $72,478 from the Capital Technology Fund for library staff laptops.
