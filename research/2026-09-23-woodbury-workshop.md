## What the record says

Woodbury City Council is scheduled for a workshop Wednesday, Sept. 23, 2026, at 5:30 p.m. in the Ash Conference Rooms at Woodbury City Hall and by virtual link. Dinner is listed at 5:00 p.m. in the Cherry Conference Room.

The workshop agenda lists two discussion items: 1A, recap and discussion of a full-scale mass casualty incident exercise, after-action review (26-178); and 1B, update of Public Safety Campus Improvements Project, construction progress and financial update (26-179).

The agenda says workshop sessions are not a public hearing, no formal votes will be taken, direction will be by consensus, and the workshop is not recorded. Unfinished workshop items will be carried over to the end of the council meeting.
