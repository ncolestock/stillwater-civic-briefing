## What the record says

The Minnesota Department of Transportation project page for the Hwy 243 Osceola Bridge, Franconia Township MN and Osceola WI, states MnDOT is working with the Wisconsin Department of Transportation to replace the Hwy 243 Osceola Bridge over the St. Croix National and Scenic Riverway. The current bridge was built in 1953. The page says a new concrete beam structure with three bridge piers will be built on the same alignment, plus a shared-use path on the north side of Hwy 243 from Osceola Landing, across the bridge, to the Village of Osceola.

Under upcoming bridge closures the page prints: Mid-October 2026 through fall 2027, "The Hwy 243 Osceola Bridge over the St. Croix River is closed." Detours printed: Hwy 8 Taylors Falls River Crossing (north of Hwy 243) via Hwy 95, Hwy 8, Hwy 35 and Hwy 243; alternate via the Hwy 36 Stillwater River Crossing (south of Hwy 243) using Hwy 95, Hwy 36, Hwy 64, Hwy 35, Hwy 64B, Hwy 35 and Hwy 243.

Current facility closure printed: 8 a.m. Mon, Sept. 14 through late summer 2028, Osceola Landing north boat launch and parking lot is closed.

The Sept. 10, 2026 MnDOT news release said MnDOT and WisDOT would host a public meeting 5 to 7 p.m. Monday, Sept. 21, at Osceola High School, 111 Oak Ridge Drive, Osceola. The meetings page lists that Sept. 21, 2026 in-person public meeting under Past. Construction completion is printed on the news release as anticipated fall 2028.
