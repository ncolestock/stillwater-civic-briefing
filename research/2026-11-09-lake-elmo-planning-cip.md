## What the record says

The Lake Elmo Planning Commission is scheduled for a public hearing on Monday, November 9, 2026, at 6:30 p.m. at Lake Elmo City Hall, 3880 Laverne Avenue North, Lake Elmo, MN 55042. The purpose is to consider the City’s 2027 – 2036 Capital Improvement Plan (CIP). All persons wishing to be heard should attend or submit comments. Written comments by mail no later than 5:00 p.m. the Wednesday before the meeting; emailed to Sophia Jensen at sjensen@lakeelmo.gov no later than noon on the day of the meeting. Materials available for review by request from Sophia Jensen, Senior City Planner (651-747-3911).
