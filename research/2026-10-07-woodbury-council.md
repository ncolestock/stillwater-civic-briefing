## What the record says

The Woodbury City Council regular meeting is scheduled October 7, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. The agenda says the meeting is taking place virtually and at Woodbury City Hall in the Council Chambers. Public comments are accepted in person. Questions between 8:00 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. at 651-714-3524 or council@woodburymn.gov. The items are scheduled. Staff recommends the actions below. No minutes of a vote on this meeting were in the pages opened.

Special order: Koins for K9s proclamation (26-192) and swearing in of new firefighter/EMS personnel Mason Mann and John Schomers (26-193). Council letter 26-192 says staff recommends the mayor adopt and sign a proclamation designating November 1 through November 30, 2026, as Koins for K-9s Month. Fiscal implications printed: "There is no fiscal implication."

Consent: approval of September 23, 2026, workshop minutes; ordinance 2084 amending Chapter 7, Land Disturbance and Erosion and Sediment Control, sections 7-3 and 7-15; lawful gambling for Destination Education a Scholarship Foundation at Mark's Cafe & Bar, 10060 City Walk Drive, resolution 26-114; and the abstract of bills.

Public hearings: keeping of chickens, ordinance 2085, project 2026-00621, amendments to Chapter 5 and Chapter 24 including Sec. 24-285 chicken coops; and USS Mazur Solar interim conditional use permit, project 25-2025-00598. The agenda says the Planning Commission reviewed the solar application September 28, 2026, and recommended approval by a 7-0 vote. Staff concur and recommend resolution 26-115, subject to conditions in council letter 26-197.

Open forum speakers are limited to three minutes, with a maximum of 20 minutes. Public-hearing speakers may not exceed 10 minutes, and the mayor may reduce time if more than three green sheets are submitted, within a 30-minute aggregate comment period.
