## Quotes

"Les Schwab Tire Center, with consent of the property owner, Oakdale Marketplace Ova LLC, requests approval for the Final Plat for Oakdale Marketplace Seventh Addition and a Development Agreement. On August 25, 2026, the developer received approvals from the City Council for the associated Preliminary Plat and Site Plan for the project. The developer’s request involves a Final Plat to re-subdivide the existing outlot into one buildable lot and two outlots for future development. On the buildable lot, the developer plans to construct an 8,200 square foot building for automotive service and repair use, primarily for the sale and installation of new tires." (packet, Final Plat section)

"The property is zoned Oakdale Marketplace PUD – Planned Unit Development. The entirety of the site is approximately 7.5 acres, with the proposed Parcel A being approximately 2.2 acres. Currently the site is vacant and is split into two sections by 33rd Street North." (packet, Final Plat section)
