## What the record says

The Oakdale City Council regular meeting is scheduled October 13, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Ave N. The items below are scheduled. Staff requests the actions named. No minutes of a vote on this meeting were in the packet. The packet also includes September 22, 2026 workshop and regular-meeting minutes for approval.

Consensus motion h asks the council to waive reading and adopt Resolution 2026-77, Amending the 2026 Streets Capital Improvement Budget for Debt Issuance – Levy. The Streets Capital Improvement Fund 2026 Budget includes $975,871 Debt Issuance – Levy as one funding source for 2026 Street Improvement Projects R2026-01 and R2026-02. Staff proposes delaying that Debt Issuance – Levy portion until 2027 and combining it with anticipated 2027 project financing. The resolution text says the council approves a revenue decrease for the 2026 Street Capital Improvement Plan Budget and, in a later whereas, to include $975,871 Debt Issuance – Levy as a funding source in 2027 as reimbursement for those 2026 projects.

Consensus motion i asks the council to waive reading and adopt Resolution 2026-76, supplementing and restating a resolution authorizing the sale of General Obligation Sales Tax and Capital Improvement (CIP) Bonds, Series 2026A, subject to parameters, and establishing a pricing committee. The council adopted a parameters resolution on September 22, 2026, for Improvement, Sales Tax Revenue, and Capital Improvement bonds, with a maximum par of $20,000,000, a maximum true interest cost of 5.5 percent, and a final maturity of February 1, 2053. Staff asks to drop the Improvement portion because of interest-rate volatility and to restate the sale under Sales Tax Revenue and Capital Improvement authority. The updated parameters printed are a maximum par of $18,600,000, a maximum true interest cost of 6 percent, and a final maturity of February 1, 2053. The proposed bond sale date is October 22, 2026. A pricing committee of the city administrator and the finance director would sign the bond purchase agreement if the best bid falls inside those parameters. The packet says a public hearing for the CIP portion was held July 23, 2024. The 2024-2028 Capital Improvement Plan estimated the city levy portion at $2 million for remaining Police Department Expansion costs and $8-10 million, in two phases, for the City Hall Remodel.

Consensus motion c asks the council to waive reading and adopt Resolution 2026-74, Approving the Final Plat for Oakdale Marketplace Seventh Addition, with conditions in the resolution, and to authorize the mayor and city administrator to sign the Development Agreement for Les Schwab Tire Center. Les Schwab Tire Center, with consent of the property owner, Oakdale Marketplace Ova LLC, requests the final plat and the agreement. On August 25, 2026, the council approved the associated preliminary plat and site plan. The request re-subdivides an existing outlot into one buildable lot and two outlots. On the buildable lot the developer plans an 8,200 square foot building for automotive service and repair, primarily sale and installation of new tires. The site is zoned Oakdale Marketplace PUD – Planned Unit Development. The site is about 7.5 acres; proposed Parcel A is about 2.2 acres. The packet says the site is vacant and is split into two sections by 33rd Street North. Parcel ID printed: 1702921410011 (north of 33rd Street N). Site access: proposed access off 33rd Street North. Comprehensive plan guidance: Mixed Use. A comprehensive plan amendment is not required. Variances requested: No. Potential deviations from city code if a PUD: No. Financial incentives requested: No. Final plats do not require Planning Commission review and go directly to the City Council. Staff recommends approval of the final plat with conditions in the resolution and approval of the development agreement.

Consensus motion b asks the council to approve charitable gambling for Knights of Columbus Council #4374 for a fundraiser on November 14, 2026, at Transfiguration Catholic Church, 6135 15th Street North. The packet says the event is a bingo fundraiser awarding cash and food items as prizes. The packet prints both the ordinance purpose and the permission rule.

Other consensus items scheduled: an agreement with Top-Tier Professional Painting LLC for pavilion painting, $37,705; appointment of Ariet Medho as a full-time probationary Human Resources Assistant on or after October 19, 2026, at a rate consistent with the city base pay schedule, conditioned on background requirements; Resolution 2026-75 accepting a $19,750 Minnesota Department of Public Safety traffic-safety enforcement grant; and an amendment to the space lease with T-Mobile Central LLC on Water Tower Three at 2347 Hallmark Avenue North, extending the agreement until January 31, 2053. The current lease, authorized September 7, 2007, commenced January 31, 2008, and is scheduled to conclude January 30, 2028. Also scheduled: First Amendment to the agreement for professional services with AE2S for the utility rate study, $9,900, for a total amended agreement amount of $109,400.

Open forum is a maximum of three minutes per person. The agenda lists a JustServe Bridge Builder Award under open forum. No public hearing is listed on the agenda cover.

The September 22, 2026 workshop minutes in this packet record council direction, not a vote, on planned-unit-development hearings. If the public hearing for new PUDs moves to the Planning Commission, residents within 500 feet of the development will be notified by the developer of a public engagement opportunity, in person or virtual. PUD amendments would move to the Planning Commission without that extra engagement. Staff was to do more research and the city attorney was to review how to put that in the code. That direction is not an item on the October 13 action agenda.
