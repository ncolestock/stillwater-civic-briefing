## What the record says

The School Board of Independent School District No. 622 (North St. Paul–Maplewood–Oakdale) is scheduled Monday, Sept. 28, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. for a regular meeting at the 622 Education Center, 2520 E. 12th Avenue, North St. Paul, MN 55109.

The BoardBook agenda lists: call to order and pledge; approval of the agenda; public comment (speakers register on a sign-up sheet, state their name, and have between two and four minutes depending on the number of speakers; the section lasts no longer than thirty minutes); consent; reports; action items; board communications; future meeting dates.

Consent: minutes of the Aug. 17, 2026 business meeting; routine personnel; disbursements (July and August 2026 monthly reports, investments, and wires); grants acceptance.

Reports: student school board representatives (Orozco/Rodriguez); summer programming (St. Michaels); sustainability initiatives update (Guyette); superintendent back-to-school/referendum updates (Tucci Osorio).

Action: acknowledgment of contributions (Livingston); resolution of proposed tax levy payable 27 (Anderson); proposed policy adoption and revisions (St. Michaels); set the location and agenda for the Oct. 5, 2026 work study session.

Future dates on the agenda: Oct. 5, 2026 work study session, 6:00 p.m.; Oct. 19, 2026 business meeting, 6:00 p.m., Boardroom.

The levy resolution document is titled "CERTIFICATION OF PROPOSED TAX LEVY / ADOPTION OF THE PROPOSED 2026 PAYABLE 2027 PROPERTY TAX LEVY." It states Minnesota Statute requires each school district to certify its proposed levy to the county auditor(s) on or before September 30 of each year; counties then develop notices of proposed taxes to be mailed by the end of November; the school board will adopt the final levy at its regular meeting in December. The Director of Finance recommends the board certify the proposed levy at the MAXIMUM. The recommended resolution: "BE IT RESOLVED by the School Board of Independent School District No. 622 that the proposed 2026 Payable 2027 property tax levy be adopted for the MAXIMUM amount and that the School Board clerk or Superintendent is authorized to sign the form necessary to certify the proposed levy to the county auditors and to the Department of Education."

The handout is titled "Proposed Levy Certification 2026 Pay 2027" and is labeled "As of Sep 21."

Policy Revisions recommends revising, to incorporate MSBA recommended changes that "reflect statutory requirements that the Minnesota legislature enacted" and "clerical updates that do not affect the substance of a policy," these numbered policies: 208 (Policy Stewardship); 402 (Disability Nondiscrimation, as printed); 416 (Drug, Alcohol, and Cannabis Testing); 423 (Employee-Student Relationships); 425 (Staff Development); 509 (Enrollment of Nonresident Students); 521 (Student Disability Nondiscrimination); 524 (Internet, Technology, and Cell Phone Acceptable Use and Safety); 613 (Graduation Requirements); 621 (Literacy and The READ Act); 624 (Online Learning Options); 709 (Student Transportation Safety); 721 (Procurement); 722 (Public Data Requests); 806 (Crisis Management).

Policy Adoption recommends adopting Policy 625, Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence, "to establish the Board's expectations for the responsible, ethical, and effective use of Artificial Intelligence throughout the district."

Grants acceptance cites Minn. Stat. 465.03 and recommends acceptance of three grants listed in Dollars.
