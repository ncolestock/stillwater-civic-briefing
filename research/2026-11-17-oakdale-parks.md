## What the record says

The Oakdale Parks and Recreation Commission meets Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, at 6:00 PM at the Oakdale Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Ave N.

The agenda includes call to order, roll, pledge, approval of agenda, approval of Sept. 15, 2026 minutes, open forum for advisory body training, review of the 2027 Draft Work Plan, staff liaison updates, commissioner updates, and council liaison updates.

## Dollars

Preliminary tax levy is 5.5%.

Upgrades for Tanners Lake Park have budgeted items primarily drawing from grants, with $500,000 coming from the Department of Natural Resources and the park preservation funds coming from developers.

Playground maintenance upgrades cost upwards of $30,000.

Pricing from the initial concept for Eberle Park has increased 20 to 30 percent from three to four years ago.
