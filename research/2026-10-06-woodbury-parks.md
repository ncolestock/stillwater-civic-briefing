## What the record says

The Woodbury Parks and Natural Resources Commission meeting is scheduled October 6, 2026, at 7 p.m. at M Health Fairview Sports Center, upper-level conference room, 4125 Radio Drive, Woodbury. The agenda says the meeting is in person only. An optional tour is listed at 6:00 p.m. at La Lake Park (South), 6748 Military Road, Woodbury. Public comments are accepted during open forum. Questions between 8 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. at 651-714-3583 or RecOnline@woodburymn.gov.

Items are scheduled, not printed as already voted. The agenda lists approval of September 8, 2026, minutes; a system-wide natural resources management plan implementation update (memorandum 26-18); Powers Lake East Park playground replacement (memorandum 26-19); discussion of park project updates, the parks and facilities long-range plan, and the 2050 comprehensive plan task force; a monthly update (memorandum 26-20); a council update from Council Member Stafford; and adjournment.

Memorandum 26-19 says an online questionnaire on the Powers Lake East Park playground replacement received 17 responses. Most respondents reported children ages 3-11 and visit at least once per week. Staff developed three playground concepts and are seeking commission feedback on which two should advance for final community review. The memo says this playground is classified as a small playground, and that the existing concrete footprint defines the available play area.

The packet also includes minutes of the September 8, 2026, commission meeting. Those minutes are a past meeting, not the October 6 action.
