This is the New Saint Croix Union for Monday, September 21. This is a short sample of the voice. It is not the weekly episode.

Washington County commissioners meet Tuesday, September 22, at nine in the morning in the County Board Room. They will take up a proposed property-tax levy of one hundred sixty-one million, nine hundred eighty-six thousand, five hundred dollars, for taxes payable in 2027. The net levy moves from one hundred forty-eight point nine million dollars to one hundred sixty point seven million, a seven point nine percent change. On a home valued at four hundred thirty thousand, six hundred dollars, the general levy, the land and water legacy line, and the railroad levy come to one thousand thirty-three dollars this year and one thousand ninety-three dollars for 2027, a difference of sixty dollars. That example assumes the home's value rose one point three percent.

The same morning includes a contract of three hundred fifty-nine thousand, seven hundred ninety-six dollars and seventy-five cents with Valley Paving, for a traffic signal where Valley Creek Road meets Eagle Creek Lane in Woodbury.

A public budget hearing is listed for December 1, after six in the evening. Final adoption is listed for December 15. Speakers fill out a comment card and are asked to finish within five minutes.
