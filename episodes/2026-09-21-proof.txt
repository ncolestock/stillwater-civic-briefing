This is the Stillwater Civic Briefing. This is a short reading from the paper, so you can hear the voice. It is not the weekly episode.

The Washington County Board meets Tuesday, September 22, at 9 in the morning. The packet asks the board to certify a proposed property tax levy of one hundred sixty-one million, nine hundred eighty-six thousand, five hundred dollars, for taxes payable in 2027. The board has not voted. A summary page in the same packet puts the net levy at about one hundred sixty point seven million dollars, up seven point nine percent from the year before.

The same agenda would also consider a contract of three hundred fifty-nine thousand, seven hundred ninety-six dollars and seventy-five cents with Valley Paving, for a traffic signal where Valley Creek Road meets Eagle Creek Lane in Woodbury.

The same afternoon, the Stillwater-area school board meets at 5:30 at the Oak Park Learning Center. The agenda asks the board to adopt a preliminary property-tax levy for taxes payable in 2027. The agenda does not print a dollar amount. It says the board may instead adopt a levy that just says maximum, so later state adjustments can still be included.
