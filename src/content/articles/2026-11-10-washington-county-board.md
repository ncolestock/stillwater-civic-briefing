Washington County commissioners meet Tuesday morning on a public hearing for fee schedule revisions and on consent items that include a $75,000 amendment for homeless prevention, a $330,000 homecare software contract, a $995,956 waste reduction grant, and $386,598 for intersection design.

The board also hears an update on the election and the Land & Water Legacy Referendum and considers a resolution setting a process for any future look at automated license plate readers. A closed session reviews a new appraisal of Phase II and Phase III of the Kelley Land and Cattle property for Big Marine Park Reserve. The agenda lists a purchase of 15085 62nd Street in Oak Park Heights for the Government Center Campus; the amount is not stated. The meeting starts at 9 a.m.
