The North St. Paul–Maplewood–Oakdale school board meets Monday at 6 p.m. to certify a proposed 2026 payable 2027 property-tax levy of $69,117,064.

That total is $876,738 below the payable-2026 figure of $69,993,802, a 1.3 percent change. The director of finance recommends certifying the proposed levy at the maximum, because final levy authority may still change as the district works with the Department of Education. The board adopts the final levy in December. Certification is due to the county auditors by Sept. 30.

The general-fund levy would fall to $35,775,982, down $2,165,965, or 5.7 percent. The community-service levy would rise to $1,745,452, up 20.9 percent. Debt service would rise to $31,595,630, up 10.9 percent. The OPEB debt-service levy would drop from $2,130,691 to zero.

Consent includes three grants: $5,000 from the Minnesota State Arts Board for the Tartan High School concert choir, $500 from the NEA, and $75,000 from the Minneapolis Foundation CAP Grant. The grant sheet quotes state law that acceptance may not be for religious or sectarian purposes.

The board is also asked to revise policies that include literacy and the READ Act, graduation requirements, enrollment of nonresident students, and internet, technology, and cell-phone use, and to adopt a new policy on responsible use of artificial intelligence throughout the district.

Speakers sign in, give their name, and have two to four minutes, depending on how many speak. The comment period lasts no longer than thirty minutes.
