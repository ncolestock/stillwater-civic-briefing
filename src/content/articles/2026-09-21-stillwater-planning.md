Stillwater's planning commission meets Wednesday at 7 p.m. at 216 4th Street North to hear a gym at 220 Commercial Street and a food truck at 225 Main Street North.

The gym would be a private membership studio for strength training, in the existing building of about 2,600 square feet. The applicant expects 15 to 20 people at a time, from 5 a.m. to 10 p.m., seven days a week. The work would be inside the building. The lot has 4 off-street spaces. The city's parking table for a health club would call for 26, and the report points to downtown street parking to cover the rest. Staff recommends approval of the conditional-use permit.

River Siren Brewing applied to park the Chuletacos food truck in the lot at 225 Main Street North. It would run from 3 p.m. to 10 p.m., up to seven days a week, and leave overnight. The Good Egg already uses that lot in the morning and ends around 2 p.m. City code allows one seasonal food truck on a property at a time. An interim-use permit is required because this truck would stay more than four days a week. The permit would last up to a year. Staff recommends approval.

Commissioners will also discuss an update to the zoning-code use tables, and outdoor commercial recreation in the Neighborhood Commercial District. The recreation item has no packet.

Open-forum remarks are limited to five minutes. A speaker at a public hearing is limited to ten.
