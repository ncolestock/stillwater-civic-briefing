The Stillwater Planning Commission is scheduled to meet Wednesday, Sept. 23, at 7 p.m. The notice says the meeting is at 216 4th Street North. Two public hearings are on the agenda.

Case CD2026-038 is a conditional-use permit request for a gym at 220 Commercial Street. Case CD2026-039 is an interim-use permit request for food vending, described on the agenda as a food truck, at 225 Main Street North.

The commission is also scheduled to discuss a zoning-code update on the use tables, with packet materials attached to the agenda, and outdoor commercial recreation uses in the Neighborhood Commercial District. The agenda says that discussion has no packet materials.

Open-forum remarks are limited to five minutes. Public-hearing remarks are limited to ten minutes. The agenda includes consideration of the Aug. 26, 2026, minutes.
