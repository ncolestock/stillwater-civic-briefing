A gym at 220 Commercial Street and a food truck at 225 Main Street North are the two public hearings before Stillwater's planning commission on Wednesday.

The gym is a conditional-use permit. The food truck is an interim-use permit for food vending. The commission has not decided either request.

Commissioners are also set to discuss a zoning-code update on the use tables. A separate discussion covers outdoor commercial recreation in the Neighborhood Commercial District.

## How to follow it

Wednesday, Sept. 23, 7 p.m., 216 4th Street North. Open forum is five minutes. A public hearing is ten.
