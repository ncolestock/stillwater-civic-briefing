Stillwater's planning commission meets Wednesday night on a zoning change that would raise the square footage allowed for accessory buildings on lots larger than 1,000 square feet.

The agenda also includes an appeal of an interpretation on accessory structures in the Cove Cottage Residential district and a conversation on the comprehensive plan's land use and density.

The commission meets at 7 p.m.
