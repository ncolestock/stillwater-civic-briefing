Washington County commissioners meet Tuesday morning for public hearings on the 2027-2031 Capital Improvement Plan and general obligation bonds under it, and on the transportation sales tax project list.

They will also discuss proposed 2027 property taxes, review recommended budget adjustments, and consider the 2027 fee schedule.

Consent items include a short-term lease at 5879 220th Street North in Forest Lake, a $1,000 donation from Hugo Yellow Ribbon Network, a $3,500 donation from Karen Marchetti for a bench at Big Marine Park Reserve, and $72,478 for library staff laptops.

The meeting is at 9 a.m. in the County Board Room at the Government Center, 14949 62nd Street North, Stillwater. The agenda does not print a dollar figure for the bond issuance or a recommended levy change.
