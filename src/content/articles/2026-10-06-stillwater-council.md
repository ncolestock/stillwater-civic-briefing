Stillwater's council meets Tuesday night on assessments for the 2026 Street Improvement Project, the 2026 Sidewalk Rehabilitation Project, and CSAH 5 Phase 4. A second hearing covers delinquent cleanup fees, citations, lodging tax, parking fees, plan review fees, solid waste fees, and utility bills. A Series 2026B bond resolution is marked available Tuesday.

Consent includes a conditional use permit for a private gym in the CBD district at 220 Commercial Street, and an interim use permit for a food truck at 225 Main Street North. A short-term rental license is listed for 225 Martha Street North. An assessment-appeal waiver is listed for a water installation at 723 William Street North.

The workshop includes a facility study and concept plans for Lily Lake Ice Arena. A closed session is listed on the assessment appeal Allan LaValier v. City of Stillwater.

A gambling premise permit is listed for Stillwater Area Hockey Assoc. at the Lumberjack Lodge. A proposed land exchange between the Department of Natural Resources and the National Park Service is on the consent list.

The workshop is at 4:30 p.m. The regular meeting is at 7 p.m.
