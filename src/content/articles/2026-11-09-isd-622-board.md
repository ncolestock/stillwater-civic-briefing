School District 622's board meets Monday evening to canvass the returns of the school district general election and to study housing, demographics, and student achievement.

The special session and the work study session both start at 6 p.m. at 2520 E. 12th Avenue in North St. Paul. The work study covers a housing and demographics study, the Comprehensive Achievement and Civic Readiness report, a superintendent check-in that includes an MCA incentive proposal, and a board check-in on officers and committees. Study sessions are open to the public but do not allow public comment.
