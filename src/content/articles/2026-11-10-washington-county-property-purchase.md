Washington County board adopted a resolution Nov. 10 to buy the property at 15085 62nd Street in Oak Park Heights for $357,700.

The purchase adds the land to the Washington County Government Center Campus in Stillwater. The campus includes the Government Center, Law Enforcement Center, Courthouse, Emergency Housing Services Building, and parking ramp. The county has bought adjacent properties over the years as they became available.

The board reviewed the proposed buy in closed session on Sept. 15. It determined the property would provide long-term value by supporting future growth of the campus. The county and the property owners agreed on the price after negotiations.

Sharon Price is the Senior Right of Way Specialist.
