Woodbury's city council meets Wednesday at 7:40 p.m., or right after a Housing and Redevelopment Authority meeting, at City Hall to set a preliminary 2027 property-tax levy of $60,855,592, including a $250,000 HRA levy.

The city levy in the resolution text is $60,605,592. The total is $593,030 below the budget-workshop figure. Staff says that change would move the estimated increase on a median-value home from $81 to $65. The estimated city tax rate would be 34.281 percent, a 3.7 percent change. The preliminary 2027 budget is $166,242,025. A Truth-in-Taxation meeting is listed for Wednesday, Dec. 9, at 7:30 p.m.

The same night includes a proposed pause, for as long as one year, on new data centers and on expansions of the existing sites at 500, 515, and 401 Bielenberg Drive. Staff says there are no current or pending applications for new data centers, and lists no budget impact.

Pulte Homes of Minnesota, LLC, asks to rezone 53 acres north of Dale Road and west of Cottage Grove Drive for a subdivision called Nottingham. The land is zoned urban reserve and guided low density. The first phase is 28 acres and 35 single-family lots. The Planning Commission recommended approval 6-0 on Sept. 14. The packet does not print lot prices.

Open-forum speakers get three minutes. Public-hearing comments may run up to 10 minutes each, with a thirty-minute aggregate period listed on the agenda.
