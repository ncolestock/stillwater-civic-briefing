Washington County commissioners meet Tuesday at 9 a.m. in the County Board Room on a resolution to set a November 17 public hearing for up to $40 million in general obligation capital improvement bonds.

The five-year Capital Improvement Plan for 2027 through 2031 would finance the South Shop Improvements Project, Park Grove Library Improvements, and R.H. Stafford Library Improvements.

The board is also asked to accept a $1,031,000 Byrne Discretionary Community Project Grant for the Sheriff's Office, covering June 1, 2026, through May 31, 2030, and to approve a $1,866,285.78 purchase order with Axon Enterprise for the period November 1, 2026, through October 31, 2031.

Consent items include a $254,002.20 purchase order with Atmosphere Commercial Interiors for Law Enforcement Center furniture and a $287,746.62 contract with Prevolv for Park Grove Library ancillary furniture.

A later workshop covers a proposed property exchange between the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources and the National Park Service for the Fairy Falls Day Use Area and the St. Croix Boom Site Wayside, plus the South Shop project.
