Stillwater City Council adopted a resolution on October 6 supporting a proposed property exchange between the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources and the National Park Service and the statutory decommissioning of the Saint Croix Boom Site Wayside.

Under the proposal, the DNR would convey the approximately 15-acre Saint Croix Boom Site Wayside to the NPS in exchange for the approximately 40-acre Fairy Falls Day Use Area. The NPS intends to manage the Boom Site in its current condition with no additional development. The DNR intends to designate and manage Fairy Falls as a Scientific and Natural Area, remaining open to the public with a focus on conservation. The resolution was adopted by the Stillwater City Council this 6th day of October, 2026. The item is on the consent agenda of the October 20 meeting.

The Saint Croix Boom Site Wayside was established by statute in 2016. The property was conveyed to the DNR from the Minnesota Department of Transportation. The site consists of three parcels totaling approximately 15 acres in Washington County. Fairy Falls Day Use Area is a 40-acre natural property currently owned by the NPS in Washington County, near the Saint Croix Boom Site Wayside.
