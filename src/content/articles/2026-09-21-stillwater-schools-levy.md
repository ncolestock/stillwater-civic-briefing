The Stillwater-area school board meets Tuesday at 5:30 p.m. at Oak Park Learning Center, 6355 Osman Avenue North, to adopt a preliminary property-tax levy for 2027. No dollar amount is printed.

The levy pays for the 2027-2028 school year. State law requires a proposed levy by Sept. 30. Levy revenue is about 29 percent of the district's general fund budget.

The board can adopt the September estimates, or it can certify the word "Maximum." A dollar amount adopted in September cannot be raised later. The presentation recommends certifying "Maximum," so later state adjustments can still be included. The board has historically adopted "Maximum" in September. After Oct. 1 the levy can only move down. Final adoption is Dec. 22.

The same meeting would set a truth-in-taxation hearing for Dec. 8 at 6 p.m. in the same building.

Consent includes a Stillwater Area High School band and orchestra trip to New Orleans on March 23-27, 2026.

The agenda includes a closed session to consider an offer to buy or sell real property.

Up to fifteen people may speak, for three minutes each. The board will not discuss individual employees or students in the public meeting.
