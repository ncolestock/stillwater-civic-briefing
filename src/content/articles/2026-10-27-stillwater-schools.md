Stillwater Area Public Schools board meets Tuesday on high school course proposals for 2027-28 and on demolishing the Old Lake Elmo School Building.

The course report covers expanded college credit, career pathways, and online learning, with one-time and ongoing costs and grant funding. Specific dollar figures are not printed in the agenda summary.

The demolition item would remove the building, underground infrastructure, and site amenities, grade the property, add topsoil, and seed it. The work is contingent on City of Lake Elmo approval.

Consent items include acceptance of a $500 grant to the Building and Grounds Department from the MASMS 2026 conference, a snowplow request for proposals, a field trip approval, and a two-year agreement with Salem Lutheran School for middle school boys’ and girls’ wrestling participation in the 2026-27 and 2027-28 school years under MSHSL Bylaw 105.00.

The meeting is at 5:30 p.m. The agenda does not print a location.
