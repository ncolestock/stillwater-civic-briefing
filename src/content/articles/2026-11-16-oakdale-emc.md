Oakdale's Environmental Management Commission meets Monday night to hear a county climate action plan and review its 2027 work plan.

Joe Ayers-Johnson and Macklyn Hutchison will present the Washington County Climate Action Plan in open forum. The commission will discuss the draft 2027 work plan. The 2026 city-wide cleanup recycled 97 percent of materials, with more mattresses, appliances, tires, electronics, and trash than in 2025.

The meeting is at 7 p.m. at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Avenue North.
