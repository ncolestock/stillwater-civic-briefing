Tuesday's agendas put a $161,986,500 county levy, a Stillwater-area school levy with no dollar printed, and $2,884,832.62 in Oakdale claims before three boards. Wednesday, Stillwater's planning commission hears a gym and a food truck.

Washington County commissioners meet at 9 a.m. in the County Board Room. The proposed levy is for taxes payable in 2027. The net levy moves from $148.9 million to $160.7 million, a 7.9 percent change. For a home valued at $430,600, the general levy, Land and Water Legacy, and the railroad levy together come to $1,033 this year and $1,093 for 2027. The morning also includes a $359,796.75 Valley Paving contract for a signal at Valley Creek Road and Eagle Creek Lane. Speakers fill out a comment card and are asked to finish within five minutes.

The Stillwater-area school board meets at 5:30 p.m. at Oak Park Learning Center, 6355 Osman Avenue North. The preliminary levy for 2027 has no dollar amount printed. The board may certify the September estimates or the word "Maximum." It has historically adopted "Maximum" in September. The same agenda would set a truth-in-taxation hearing for Dec. 8 at 6 p.m. in that building. Up to fifteen speakers get three minutes each.

Oakdale's council meets at 7 p.m. at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Avenue North. P 4 Properties LLC wants a three-story self-storage building on a vacant lot at 6815 55th Street North. The lot is zoned general industrial, where self-storage is not allowed, so the request includes a rezoning to light industrial. Claims from Sept. 9 through Sept. 22 come to $2,884,832.62. Open-forum speakers get three minutes.

The planning commission meets Wednesday at 7 p.m. at 216 4th Street North. The hearings are a gym at 220 Commercial Street and a food truck at 225 Main Street North. A hearing speaker is limited to ten minutes.
