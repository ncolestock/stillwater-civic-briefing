Stillwater's council meets Monday night on bids for the Shoddy Mill Bergstein Warehouse Project and a task order for PFAS treatment at Well 10.

The agenda also lists a second reading of a zoning text amendment for accessory structures in the Traditional Residential district, liquor and tobacco license renewals for 2027, and a temporary liquor license for the Episcopal Church of the Ascension.

Recognitions include oaths for Officers Troy Edmond, Shawn Milian, and Sean Dobb. Sergeant Stephen Papke and Police Chief Brian Mueller are named in the awards.

The meeting is at 7 p.m. at City Hall. The agenda does not print dollar amounts for the contracts or the task order.
