Washington County's library board meets Monday from 6:30 to 8 p.m. at R.H. Stafford Library in Woodbury to hear a 2027 budget presentation that lists a county net levy of $10,918,800.

That levy line moves from $10,336,200 in 2026, a 5.64 percent change. Non-levy revenue is listed at $634,900, up from $402,100. Personnel would be $7,785,900, services and charges $1,993,300, and materials and supplies $1,774,500. The agenda calls the item a budget presentation.

The same meeting includes a Strategic Facilities Plan workshop with MSR Design and a construction update on Park Grove and R.H. Stafford. July 27 minutes in the packet say Park Grove demolition is complete, the entry foundation is in, and water damage led to replacing all exterior walls while keeping structural steel and the roof. The packet does not print the MSR contract amount or construction bid totals.

The meeting is in Savannah A, lower level, inside Central Parks.
