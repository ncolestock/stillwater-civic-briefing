Woodbury's council meets Wednesday on an interim solar permit and on rules for keeping chickens.

The planning commission reviewed the USS Mazur Solar application September 28 and recommended approval, 7-0. Staff concur and recommend the permit, subject to conditions in the council letter. US Solar wants a community solar garden at 11860 Dale Road Court, panels on about 27 acres, north of Dale Road and west of Manning Avenue. The property is zoned R-1, Urban Reserve.

A hearing the same night covers amendments on chicken coops.

Bills of $1,476,841.44 from September 11 and $4,418,562.05 from September 18 are up for approval.

Destination Education a Scholarship Foundation is up for lawful gambling at Mark's Cafe & Bar, 10060 City Walk Drive.

A proclamation would name November 1 through November 30, 2026, Koins for K-9s Month. The letter says there is no fiscal implication. Donna Smith-Stafford started the Woodbury K9 Fund. Mason Mann and John Schomers are listed for swearing-in as firefighter and EMS personnel.

The meeting is at 7:30 p.m., virtual and in the Council Chambers at Woodbury City Hall. Comments in person are accepted. Open forum speakers get three minutes, inside a 20-minute cap. Public-hearing speakers may not exceed 10 minutes. If more than three green sheets come in, the mayor may shorten that time, inside a 30-minute total.
