Oakdale's council meets Tuesday on a public hearing to certify $248,503.75 in delinquent utilities and inactive accounts onto 2027 property taxes.

Three hundred two accounts make up the total, including interest and a $35 certification fee on each. Remaining balances would be levied with 5.75 percent interest. Payments are accepted until November 15.

The same night the council takes up a $141,280 professional services agreement with KLM Engineering for Water Tower 4 reconditioning, a $167,452.75 change order for Walton Park parking lot work on the police expansion and city hall remodel, and claims of $5,685,044.07 covering October 14 through 27.

Debt service funds that finished paying off in 2025 and 2026 would close. Cash balances of $44,964, $30,735, and $272,666 would move to the closed bond fund.

A joint powers agreement would let the owner of property at 4455 Olson Lake Trail pay a $25,869 lateral benefit charge for sanitary sewer, with no city expense. A memorandum of understanding with the Ramsey-Washington Metro Watershed District estimates the city's portion of sediment removal at $250,000, with $200,000 from the 2026-2030 capital improvement plan.

The meeting is at 7 at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Avenue North.
