The Highway 243 bridge between Franconia Township and Osceola closes mid-October 2026 through fall 2027 while Minnesota and Wisconsin replace the 1953 span.

The new bridge is a concrete beam structure with three piers on the same alignment, plus a shared-use path on the north side of Hwy 243 from Osceola Landing, across the river, to the Village of Osceola.

The north detour is the Hwy 8 crossing at Taylors Falls, by Hwy 95, Hwy 8, Hwy 35 and Hwy 243. The south detour is the Hwy 36 Stillwater crossing, by Hwy 95, Hwy 36, Hwy 64, Hwy 35, Hwy 64B, Hwy 35 and Hwy 243.

Osceola Landing's north boat launch and parking lot have been closed since 8 a.m. Monday, Sept. 14, through late summer 2028.

The project page and the Sept. 10 news release do not state a cost. Construction is anticipated to finish in fall 2028. The Federal Highway Administration's finding, printed on the project page, is that the work will have no significant impact on the environment.
