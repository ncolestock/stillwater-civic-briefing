Washington County commissioners meet Tuesday on a $1,244,000 Environment and Natural Resources Trust Fund grant for the Hardwood Creek Regional Trail Extension. The consent calendar also covers Local Road Improvement Program applications, a letter of support for a state-federal property exchange, and several other contract amendments.

The grant is Agreement No. 297766 with the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources. The board would act as state aid sponsor for 2026 LRIP applications that include a 2.7-mile rehabilitation of 30th Street North in West Lakeland Township, a 1.6-mile rehabilitation of Stonebridge Trail North in Stillwater Township, and a 1.1-mile rehabilitation of McKusick Road North in the City of Grant.

A letter of support would back a proposed property exchange and statutory decommissioning between the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources and the National Park Service involving the Fairy Falls Day Use Area and the St. Croix Boom Site Wayside.

Other consent items include a $211,494.98 purchase order with Insight Public Sector Inc. for network infrastructure, a $63,100 Enforcement Grant from the Minnesota Department of Public Safety, a $5,000 donation from Joshua’s Hope, an amendment adding $310,272 to a Bridges II housing program contract (total $465,408), an amendment adding $1,382,956 to a Mobile Crisis Grant, a 10 percent administrative fee of $7,777 on a non-emergency medical transportation agreement with Hennepin County, and a $10,084 Justice Assistance Grant application for respirator filters.

A workshop with administration follows on the 2027 Proposed Fee Schedule. Black-and-white photocopies would rise from 10 cents to 15 cents a page. Color photocopies would drop from 70 cents to 50 cents. Public Health regulatory program fees would increase 5 percent. Other proposed changes cover parks vehicle permits, camping, pavilion and facility rentals, sheriff civil process fees and fingerprint cards (from $15 to $20), and temporary access permits. A public hearing on the fees is scheduled for November 10.

The regular meeting starts at approximately 10:00 a.m. The agenda does not print a meeting place.
