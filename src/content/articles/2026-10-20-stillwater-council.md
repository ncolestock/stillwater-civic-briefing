Stillwater's council meets Tuesday night for public hearings on proposed assessments for street and sidewalk projects and for delinquent fees.

It will also consider a resolution for 2026 general obligation bonds, a feasibility study for the 2027 street improvement project, and a land exchange with the National Park Service.

The council meets at 7 p.m. at Stillwater City Hall, 216 Fourth Street North. A workshop begins at 4:30 p.m.
