Oakdale's council meets Tuesday night on claims of $2,838,563.15 and a feasibility study for street reconstruction and overlay work.

The claims cover October 28 through November 10. An agreement with SEH Inc. for the feasibility report is not to exceed $44,112.

From the October 27 workshop, proposed 2027 utility rates would raise a small residential bill by $2.64 a month and a large residential bill by $8.48 a month. A large commercial customer with a 3-inch meter would see $101.24 more a month.

The packet notes city costs for the County Highway 13 project in a range around $293,000 to $500,000. Street projects in the capital plan are listed at $6.2 million for reconstruction and $2.62 million for overlay.

The council is also set to canvass the November 3 general election returns and to consider a resolution of support for the County Highway 13 improvement project.

A $50 Dunkin’ gift card donation for the Fire Department is on the agenda. An Accurint agreement is listed at $6,000 a year.

The meeting is at 7 p.m. at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Avenue North.
