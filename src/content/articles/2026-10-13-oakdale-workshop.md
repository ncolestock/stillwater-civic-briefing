Oakdale's council workshop Tuesday is on an 8-acre Helmo Station site the Metropolitan Council owns beside the Gold Line station. A joint-powers draft would not approve a development.

The land sits in the Helmo Station planned-unit district. The Metropolitan Council acquired it for the Gold Line. A warehouse that stood there has been demolished. About one acre on the south is a park-and-ride. About seven acres are left for redevelopment. On December 9, 2025, the council told staff to keep the planned-unit district as the framework and work up a concept plan.

The draft agreement is among the city, the city's Economic Development Authority, and the Metropolitan Council. It does not approve a developer, a financing package, or a property conveyance. Zoning and development approvals stay with the city. A later project would still need the usual council vote. The selected developer would pay market value to the Metropolitan Council unless a different price is approved under the legal and funding rules. The agreement would not require the city to pay for future infrastructure. Staff's timeline puts a council vote on the agreement at November 10.

Oakdale Summerfest Community Organization voted to keep the festival if security and funding can be worked out. Larry Eberhard, Mary Koonce, and Pam Jungmann are listed to talk through 2026 and what 2027 would take.

Tanners Lake Park is up for direction on 2027 work, a playground theme, and lighting. The 2024 lighting study uses a 13-foot post-top unit, a concrete pole, and a King Acorn fixture. On average those lights sit 240 feet apart, which the memorandum calls non-continuous lighting. Continuous lighting would need more fixtures and would cost more. The Acorn fixture is not DarkSky Approved because of its lens. A street-lighting policy in the packet prints about $13,000 per pole in 2022, subject to inflation.

July and September workshops directed an increase to $25 a meeting for the chair and $20 for each member.

The workshop is at 5 p.m. at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Avenue North.
