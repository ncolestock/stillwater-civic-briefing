Oakdale's Environmental Management Commission meets October 19 at 7 p.m. at the Oakdale Discovery Center on a $40 million bond referendum the county is proposing for the November 3 election.

Serena Raths and Emily Jorgensen of Washington County will present on the Land and Water Legacy Program. The program was created in November 2006 with a $20 million voter-approved referendum for land and water protection. Over $53 million has been invested, $19 million from county funds and $34 million from partnership funds.

The proposed 2026 bonds would cost an estimated $24.60 a year for 20 years on a median residential homestead valued at $420,900. Agricultural homesteads would see $3 per $100,000 of taxable value. Agricultural non-homesteads would see $6 per $100,000.
