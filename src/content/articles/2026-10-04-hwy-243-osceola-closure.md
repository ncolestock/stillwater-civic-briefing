The Hwy 243 Osceola Bridge over the St. Croix River is scheduled to close in late October 2026 through fall 2027.

Minnesota Department of Transportation is working with the Wisconsin Department of Transportation to replace the bridge, built in 1953, over the St. Croix National and Scenic Riverway.

Detours run north on the Hwy 8 Taylors Falls River Crossing via Hwy 95, Hwy 8, Hwy 35 and Hwy 243, or south on the Hwy 36 Stillwater River Crossing using Hwy 95, Hwy 36, Hwy 64, Hwy 35, Hwy 64B, Hwy 35 and Hwy 243.

The Osceola Landing north boat launch and parking lot is closed from 8 a.m. Monday, Sept. 14 through late summer 2028.

No dollar amount appears on the project page.

The purpose is to maintain a reliable highway connection over the St. Croix River for Hwy 243 between Washington and Chisago counties in Minnesota and the Village of Osceola and Polk County in Wisconsin.
