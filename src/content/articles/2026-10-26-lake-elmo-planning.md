Lake Elmo's Planning Commission meets Monday on a variance for a new 872-square-foot detached garage at 11055 32nd Street North.

Doug and Kelly Biever want to construct the garage, remove the existing 579-square-foot structure and other impervious surface, and relocate the driveway. The lot is 0.6 acres zoned Rural Single Family with a Shoreland Overlay. City code sets a maximum impervious surface coverage of 25 percent. The property already exceeds that limit: 30.9 percent now, after earlier renovations that left it at 32.9 percent and 33.8 percent before that.

Staff recommends approval with conditions that keep the finished coverage at 28.6 percent, require the existing impervious to be removed first, and call for materials that match the house plus a vegetative buffer review with the Washington County Conservation District. The variance would expire if work does not start within 12 months.

The existing garage is too small and angled, forcing maneuvering onto neighboring property. Boat storage is common for lakeshore property but restricted in front yards.

The commission meets at 6:30 p.m. at 3880 Laverne Avenue North.
