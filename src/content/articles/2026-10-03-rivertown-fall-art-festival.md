The 49th Rivertown Fall Art Festival fills Lowell Park in downtown Stillwater on Saturday, Oct. 3, and Sunday, Oct. 4. Hours are 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. both days. The Greater Stillwater Chamber of Commerce puts it on along the St. Croix at 201 Water Street.

It is a juried fine-art fair. The chamber says more than 200 artists and makers will show handcrafted work in mediums that include painting, photography, jewelry, ceramics, glass, fiber, metal, wood, and mixed media.

Besides the booths, the chamber lists a beer-and-wine tent, local food vendors, and live music through the weekend. As of the chamber’s 2026 pages, the music schedule and the named food-vendor list were not posted yet.

The chamber puts expected attendance above 10,000. Lowell Park sits next to Main Street shops and restaurants. The city calendar matches the same weekend hours and park address.
