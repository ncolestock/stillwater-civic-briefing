The 49th Rivertown Fall Art Festival is at Lowell Park in Stillwater on Oct. 3 and 4. Hours are 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. both days.

The Greater Stillwater Chamber of Commerce puts it on at 201 Water Street. The city calendar lists a beverage tent, food, and live music with the art.
