Lake Elmo's Planning Commission holds a public hearing Monday, November 9, on the city's 2027–2036 Capital Improvement Plan.

The hearing is at 6:30 p.m. at Lake Elmo City Hall, 3880 Laverne Avenue North. All persons wishing to be heard should attend or submit comments. Written comments by mail no later than 5:00 p.m. the Wednesday before the meeting; emailed to Sophia Jensen at sjensen@lakeelmo.gov no later than noon on the day of the meeting. Materials available for review by request from Sophia Jensen, Senior City Planner.

Specific dollar figures, project list, or full CIP document are not in the notice.
