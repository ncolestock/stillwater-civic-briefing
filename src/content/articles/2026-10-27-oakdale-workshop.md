Oakdale's council workshop Tuesday night takes up a proposed $22,143,344 property-tax levy for 2027, an 8.97 percent increase of $1,823,616.

The general fund accounts for $1,163,851 of the rise, the capital improvement program $523,000, and debt service $169,765. A median residential property valued at $352,000 would see about $110.42 more a year, or $9.20 a month. Low-volume residential utility bills would rise $2.64 a month and high-volume bills $8.48.

The workshop also covers a utility rate study, value engineering on the police expansion and city hall remodel, the proposed 2027 fee schedule, the 2027-2031 capital improvement plan, and 2027-2036 vehicle and equipment replacements. The vehicle and equipment replacement fund levy would be $1,100,000, a 4.76 percent increase. The municipal building fund would rise to $593,000, a 747.14 percent increase. Park capital would be $407,000.

Enterprise fund transfers under discussion include $673,014 from water and $634,200 from sewer. A $1 million transfer from the Tanners Lake tax-increment financing internal financing loan is listed for park improvements.

Truth-in-Taxation is set for December 8 at 7 p.m., the same night the final levy would be adopted.

The workshop starts at 5 at the Discovery Center, 4444 Hadley Avenue North.
