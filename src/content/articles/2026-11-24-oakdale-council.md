Oakdale's council meets Tuesday night on a comprehensive plan change, PUD amendment, plat, and site plan for land at 5585 Golfview Avenue North and an adjacent parcel.

The items cover a public hearing and recommendations to adopt a resolution for the comprehensive plan amendment, an ordinance amending the Greens of Silver Lake PUD to allow residential use, and resolutions for the preliminary plat and site plan. The council will also consider claims totaling $3,350,906.95 for mid-November, a $55,000 developer contribution toward Highway 120 improvements, and a $1,000,000 Metropolitan Council grant for 72-unit affordable housing at 6700 Hudson Boulevard North.

Staff is recommending denial of a billboard text amendment and variance at the Tanners Lake PUD. Other items include consensus motions and a zoning ordinance text amendment for PUDs.

The meeting is at 7 p.m. at the Discovery Center on Hadley Avenue North.
