Stillwater's planning commission meets Wednesday night on a zoning change that would let accessory structures reach 1,000 square feet on parcels of 50,000 square feet or more in the Traditional Residential district.

Daniel Garrity wants a 1,000 square foot accessory structure at 12210 McKusick Road, on top of a proposed attached garage. Current rules cap an attached garage plus all accessory structures at 1,000 square feet or 10 percent of the net developable area, whichever is less. An accessory structure itself is limited to 500 square feet, or 720 when no garage is attached. Staff proposes the larger allowance and a 1,500 square foot combined maximum for those big lots. The property is about 1.7 acres. Only three parcels would be affected. The application was complete September 21. The 60-day deadline is November 20.

Gina and Dan Sedlacek appeal the staff view that a pool is an accessory structure, and therefore barred, at 1234 Macey Way in Settler's Glen. City code says accessory structures are not allowed in the Cove Cottage Residential district. Three existing pools in Settlers Glen were approved administratively in the last five years.

Staff will also discuss land use and density for the 2050 comprehensive plan. The updated map must show that land expected to develop in the next 20 years can hold the growth forecasts from the Metropolitan Council. Those forecasts put population at 22,500 in 2050, up from 19,394 in 2020; households at 9,600 from 7,750; employment at 12,500 from 7,974.

The commission meets at 7 p.m. at 216 4th Street North.
