Oakdale's city council meets Tuesday night on a public hearing to certify delinquent utility accounts to the 2027 property tax rolls.

The same agenda includes a $141,280 agreement with KLM Engineering for reconditioning Water Tower 4 and a joint powers agreement letting a property at 4455 Olson Lake Trail connect to Oakdale's sanitary sewer.

The meeting is at 7 at the Discovery Center on Hadley Avenue.
