Oakdale's Parks and Recreation Commission meets Tuesday night to review the 2027 draft work plan, which includes a 5.5 percent preliminary tax levy.

Upgrades for Tanners Lake Park draw primarily from grants, including $500,000 from the Department of Natural Resources, with park preservation funds from developers. Playground maintenance upgrades cost upwards of $30,000. Pricing for the initial concept for Eberle Park has increased 20 to 30 percent from three to four years ago.

The commission meets at 6 p.m. at the Oakdale Discovery Center on Hadley Avenue. The agenda also includes approval of the Sept. 15 minutes and an open forum for advisory body training.
