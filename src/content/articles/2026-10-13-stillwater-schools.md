Stillwater's school board meets Tuesday on this year's reading, math, and science results, and on a high school schedule the district already put in place.

The achievement report covers the district's goals under the state's Comprehensive Achievement and Civic Readiness law, and 2025-26 Minnesota Comprehensive Assessment results in reading, mathematics, and science. The agenda marks it information only.

Stillwater Area High School started a redesigned schedule this school year to add courses and give students more flexibility. The presentation covers early results, who is in the new programming, and where the district sees room to improve. A course-proposal discussion is on the same list. The final course list goes to the October business meeting for review and approval.

A part-time teacher program resolution is on the agenda for review. Kristine Carlston, executive director of human resources, is the speaker.

Consent includes an Eagle Scout project to build wooden picnic benches for Stillwater Middle School, estimated at $800 in materials. Invoices from September 19 to October 9 are also on consent. The agenda says that register was distributed to board members.

A closed session is listed to develop or consider an offer to buy or sell real property.

The study session is at 5:30 p.m. at Oak Park Learning Center, 6355 Osman Avenue North.
