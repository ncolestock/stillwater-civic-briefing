Lake Elmo's city council holds a public hearing Tuesday on a demolition and grading permit for the old Lake Elmo Elementary and on a business subsidy policy.

Stillwater Area Schools, ISD 834, seeks to demolish the old school building at 11030 Stillwater Boulevard North. The request covers removal of the structure, parking, drive and related improvements, earthwork, and vegetative restoration. The work would move about 525 cubic yards of earth per acre.

The hearing also considers a business subsidy policy. The policy would set minimum requirements and standards that recipients must meet to receive a business subsidy.

The hearing is at 7 p.m. at Lake Elmo City Hall, 3880 Laverne Avenue North. Materials are available by request to the senior city planner.
