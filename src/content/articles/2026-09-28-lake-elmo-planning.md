Lake Elmo's planning commission meets Monday at 6:30 p.m. at 3880 Laverne Avenue North on a comprehensive-plan change at 4455 Olson Lake Trail North and a variance at 11055 32nd Street North.

Lisa Smith asks to expand the city's MUSA boundary, served by Oakdale, and re-guide 4455 Olson Lake Trail North to RSFS. The agenda says the reason is a noncompliant septic system.

Doug Biever asks for flexibility from the 25 percent impervious-surface cap at 11055 32nd Street North to build a new garage and move the driveway.

The commission is also asked to report that a sale of city property in the old village, including the old fire hall and parks buildings (PIDs 1302921320100 and 1302921320099), would comply with the comprehensive plan. The agenda does not print a sale price or a buyer.

The Oct. 14 meeting is canceled. The next listed meeting is Oct. 26.
