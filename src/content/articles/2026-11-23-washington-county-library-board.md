The Washington County Library Board meets Monday on a construction update for the Park Grove and R.H. Stafford libraries and on dates for its 2027 meetings.

The board gathers from 6:30 to 8:00 p.m. at Oakdale Library, 1010 Heron Avenue North in Oakdale. The agenda includes a consent item for the September 28 minutes, the construction update from Capital Project Manager Erik Jalowitz, 2027 meeting dates from Library Director Jacquie Kramer, statistics and updates from Kramer, and an Oakdale Library update.

The agenda does not print dollar figures for the construction work.
