Stillwater Area Public Schools meets Tuesday evening to canvass the general election returns and to act on a high school course proposal that expands college credit, career pathways, and online options for 2027-28.

The meeting starts at 5:30 p.m. at Oak Park Learning Center, 6355 Osman Avenue North. The board will also take up a resolution authorizing certificates of the election. A workshop covers enrollment, transportation, and child care. A closed session will consider an offer to buy or sell real property.
