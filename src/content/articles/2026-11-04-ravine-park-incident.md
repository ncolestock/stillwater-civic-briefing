Cottage Grove police and the Washington County Sheriff’s Office responded about 6:11 a.m. on Nov. 4 to a report of shots fired in Ravine Park.

Officers found two people with gunshot wounds. Both were pronounced dead at the scene.

The preliminary investigation indicates the two were known to each other and that the shooting was not a random act of violence. There is no active threat to the community.

Ravine Park remains closed until further notice. The Washington County Service Center was closed for the day. Cottage Grove City Hall and the HERO Center have reopened.

The Ramsey County Medical Examiner’s Office will identify the people and notify next of kin.
