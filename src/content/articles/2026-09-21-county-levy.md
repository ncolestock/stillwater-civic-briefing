Washington County commissioners are set Tuesday to certify a proposed $161,986,500 property-tax levy for 2027. They have not voted yet.

A summary page puts the net levy at $148.9 million for 2026 and $160.7 million as the 2027 estimate. That is a 7.9 percent change. The staff report recommends a 4.7 percent levy change for the Land and Water Legacy Program.

A separate draft would certify a Regional Railroad Authority levy of $660,000. The staff report says the railroad levy does not change. Another draft would consent to a Community Development Agency levy not to exceed $7,283,446.

The staff report says certification is due on or before Sept. 30. In that paragraph the report refers to taxes payable in 2026. The draft resolutions are titled for taxes payable in 2027.

Also Tuesday: a $359,796.75 contract with Valley Paving for a traffic signal at Valley Creek Road and Eagle Creek Lane. Two agreements with Woodbury cover the construction share and later maintenance.

The packet lists a public budget hearing on Dec. 1 after 6 p.m. Final adoption of the levy is listed for Dec. 15.
