Washington County commissioners meet Tuesday at 9 a.m. in the County Board Room to certify a proposed property-tax levy of $161,986,500 for taxes payable in 2027.

The net levy moves from $148.9 million in 2026 to $160.7 million in 2027, a 7.9 percent change. The net tax rate moves from 24.45 percent to 25.52 percent. For a residential homestead valued at $430,600, the estimate adds the general levy, the Land and Water Legacy line, and the railroad levy: $1,033 this year and $1,093 for 2027, a difference of $60. The example assumes the home's value rose 1.3 percent.

The Land and Water Legacy amount moves from $1.2 million to $1.3 million. The railroad levy stays $660,000. A Community Development Agency levy would be capped at $7,283,446.

The same morning includes a traffic signal at Valley Creek Road and Eagle Creek Lane in Woodbury. Valley Paving was the low bidder at $359,796.75. The engineer's estimate was $556,613.20. Woodbury would share the construction cost and the later maintenance.

Certification is due Sept. 30. A public budget hearing is listed for Dec. 1, after 6 p.m. Final adoption of the levy, the budget, and the capital plan is listed for Dec. 15.
