Oakdale's council meets Tuesday on a 55-unit rental townhome project that needs a comprehensive plan amendment, a PUD change, a preliminary plat, and a site plan.

Mulcahy Holdings LLC wants the homes at Cedric's Crossing. The site plan includes a $55,000 contribution toward Highway 120 improvements. Planning Commission recommended approval on October 1.

Claims for November 11 through November 24 total $3,350,906.95. A Metropolitan Council Livable Communities Act grant of $1,000,000 would go to Hudson Boulevard Apartments, a 72-unit affordable project at 6700 Hudson Boulevard North, as a deferred loan with no local match.

Tanners Lake TIF District No. 2-12 holds a $2.3 million surplus that would return to the county, the school district, and the city by December 31, 2027, if the district is decertified. A billboard zoning text amendment and variance in the Tanners Lake PUD is recommended for denial.

The meeting is at 7 p.m. at the Discovery Center on Hadley Avenue.
