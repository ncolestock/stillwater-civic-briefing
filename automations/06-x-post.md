## What to do

1. Read `src/content/articles/` changed in the last 24 hours. Keep files whose status is published. Skip draft and held.
2. If there are none, stop. Do not post.
3. For each new published article, post once. The post is the headline, the city, one sentence copied from the article, and the article URL. Under 280 characters.
4. Include the words "AI, from the public record." If the article is a blotter item, add "Allegations, not convictions."
5. If the X connector is not connected, do not pretend you posted. Email the drafts to nathan0colestock@gmail.com instead.
6. Email an eight-line note of what you posted or drafted.

## Do not

- Post a draft or a held story. The fact-check desk has not passed it.
- Name a host. Do not use a personal voice.
- Add a fact, dollar, or charge that is not in the article.
- Post a home address, age, mugshot, or the name of a juvenile.
- Post the same article twice. A correction gets one new post that says it is a correction and links the article.
- Quote another newspaper.
